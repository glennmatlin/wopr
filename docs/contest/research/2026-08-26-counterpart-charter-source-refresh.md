Status: Milestone 5 evidence note for the candidate Himaldesh and Olvana Charters

Snapshot date: 2026-08-26

## Scope and method

I refreshed the official DATE pages needed to define the minimum episode-relevant Counterpart Rooms. I separated DATE statements from the machine procedure inferred for this exercise. I did not treat a named institution as a complete decision rule, infer a missing party roster, or convert a strategic posture into a runnable action route.

ODIN describes DATE as a living training product. The candidates therefore bind retrieval dates and source identities, and a changed source requires review rather than silent replacement.

## Nuclear-source correction

The current Olvana military page is dated 2026-01-13. It describes public non-initiation and possible nuclear use if Olvana faces certain defeat with expected regime change or is attacked by a nuclear power. This refresh did not recover the older preserved wording about some policymakers considering a small coercive nuclear use.

I therefore treat the older limited-use statement as unresolved source drift. The candidate does not make that debate available to a Setup or Room, and it blocks every Olvanan strategic or nuclear action because the sources do not supply a complete forum, information path, confirmation map, authenticated route, or effect authority. Nuclear risk remains part of the arena, but this candidate cannot turn that risk into an unsupported runnable option.
