# Define authenticity as inspectable fidelity

Status: accepted scope clarification D61; refines ADRs 0010, 0025, and 0033

The primary simulator remains a DATE-backed conflict between Himaldesh and Olvana with nuclear escalation reachable but not scripted. The complete U.S. Situation Room is the focal evaluated instrument responding to that conflict. The project's high-authenticity aspiration will be represented as inspectable institutional, informational, causal, and evidentiary fidelity rather than asserted as realism or predictive validity.

## Considered options

- Maximize surface military detail and real-unit resemblance.
- Use open-ended model interaction without a typed causal or institutional contract.
- Treat Nuclear War as the primary simulation and DATE as supporting flavor.
- Begin with an imminent nuclear-use decision, or disable the nuclear path entirely.
- Pursue public-source institutional fidelity, coherent fictional operations, open policy, state-bounded consequences, and complete replay in the primary DATE crisis.

Surface detail does not make decision authority, uncertainty, or consequences authentic and creates avoidable real-world and claim risk. Unbounded interaction preserves creativity but cannot distinguish causal World state from persuasive prose. A Nuclear War-first entry loses the intended realistic crisis simulator. An immediate nuclear choice crowds out conventional crisis work, while a disabled path removes the contest's nuclear-risk subject. The selected target keeps the LLM nuclear-simulation genre's strategic openness while making the Room and World inspectable.

## Consequences

DATE facts and public official sources must remain distinct from design inferences. Actor-specific Charters must control groups, mandates, information, activation, authority, and dissent. Operational labels and Force Packages remain fictional but must support coherent crisis and escalation reasoning. Nuclear warning, readiness, signaling, misinterpretation, or use may emerge only through authored state, actor authority, Room decisions, and admitted consequences.

Public evidence must expose manifests, source cards, selective information, event lineage, decisions, dissent, EXCON proposals, validator results, and replay. The Packet may describe unusually high authenticity as the design target and explain its mechanisms, but it must not claim superior realism, predictive accuracy, or comparative quality until receipt-bound evidence supports the narrower claim. Nuclear War remains a deliberately unrealistic secondary cross-World stressor for the same U.S. Room.
