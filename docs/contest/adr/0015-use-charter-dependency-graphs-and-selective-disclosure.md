# Use Charter dependency graphs and selective disclosure

Status: proposed normalization of locked decisions; wording awaits owner ratification

All actors share one outer crisis cycle, but each Room executes its own Charter-defined group dependency graph. Independent groups may run concurrently after their inputs are frozen; shared seats and product dependencies create serialization barriers. A persistent seat cannot be cloned into simultaneous groups, and canonical Charter order, not provider completion time, determines the retained record.

Role-private information remains private until a logged Charter-permitted disclosure. Every active seat receives the common information it is entitled to plus only its private brief and prior disclosures; no adapter may build a union prompt and rely on instructions to simulate privacy.

## Considered options

- One global plenary conversation.
- A fixed serial schedule for every actor.
- Unbounded asynchronous discussion.
- Automatic pooling or permanent compartmentalization of all briefs.

The selected graph preserves institutional structure, safe concurrency, and inspectable information flow without letting latency or prompt leakage change the decision procedure.
