# Three full-press Presets; third rule still unnamed

Status: accepted historical decision; superseded by the locked DATE pivot recorded in draft ADR-0010

The Sounding uses three Presets, all with full press. Two of them are sole release and council. The third is a distinct Release rule, not a press switch and not a mixed-structure game.

The label "two-person" is not accepted yet. The implemented `distributed` rule is pre-delegated any-holder release. A true two-person rule (two holders must pick the same action) is not on the contest path. Council at a two-thirds threshold already requires two of three votes.

Consequence: PROTOCOL stays unamended until the third Preset's rule is named. Adding `distributed` to the Concordia contest allow-list is required only if the third Preset is pre-delegated.
