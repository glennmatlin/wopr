# End the first episode after two complete U.S. Room Cycles

Status: accepted decision D53; refined by D54; partially resolves OPEN-WORLD-002 and OPEN-RUN-003

The first DATE episode has a normal Episode Horizon of exactly two complete U.S. Room Cycles. Cycle 1 begins at STARTEX and closes only after the U.S. decision, its Open Action Proposals or recorded non-action, World adjudication and validation, and the resulting information state return to the U.S. Room. Cycle 2 begins from that changed information state, requires an attributable reassessment and second decision, and closes after its proposals or non-action are adjudicated, final consequences are returned, and the Core, ledger, and evaluation views are frozen.

## Considered options

- End after two complete U.S. decision and consequence cycles.
- Run to a fixed simulated time while allowing the number of completed Room Cycles to vary.
- Continue until withdrawal, recapture, ceasefire, wider war, or another strategic resolution.

A fixed time alone can give compared Rooms unequal opportunities to decide and adapt. Playing to strategic resolution makes duration, model-call cost, and institutional exposure depend too heavily on the trajectory and shifts attention toward who obtained a final outcome. Two cycles directly exercise the selected information-to-decision-to-consequence-to-adaptation claim within a bounded run.

## Consequences

The second U.S. decision may reaffirm, modify, narrow, expand, withdraw, condition, or decline the first policy, but it must explicitly reassess the changed evidence, World state, dependencies, and unresolved risk. Repetition without an attributable reassessment is not an adaptive cycle. A decided non-action or delay remains sufficient when it follows the complete Room path and the World adjudicates its consequences.

The two-cycle horizon is not a victory condition and does not require the ridge crisis to be resolved. A frozen substantive World terminal may end a run earlier and remains a valid outcome; an execution or artifact abort remains invalid and MUST NOT be scored as a World result. An early substantive terminal does not by itself supply the Packet's required completed two-cycle matched comparison. The horizon and terminal rules are fixed across matched runs, while counterpart Rooms may take the decisions their Charters and the MSEL require without being forced into two symmetric cycles.

Fixed-time and play-to-resolution horizons remain available as separately declared run or evaluation profiles, not Crisis Setups or Seeds. D54 supplies the shared Cycle 2 pressure through one Matched Pressure Inject plus separately retained endogenous consequences. Exact cycle call budgets, internal turns, event timing, terminal predicates, and abort codes remain implementation and Setup-authoring work.
