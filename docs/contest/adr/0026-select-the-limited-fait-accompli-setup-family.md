# Select the limited fait-accompli family for the first Crisis Setup

Status: proposed normalization of locked decision D41; wording awaits owner ratification

The first Crisis Setup must belong to `ridge_seizure.limited_fait_accompli`. At World ground truth, Olvana's central leadership authorizes seizure of a fictional high-altitude ridge and adjacent logistics node, intends to hold the position for a bounded territorial advantage, and expects to avoid general war. The U.S. Room does not receive that intent automatically.

## Considered options

- `ridge_seizure.resolve_probe`, in which the position is expendable and the primary objective is testing U.S. and Himaldeshi resolve.
- `ridge_seizure.local_exploitation`, in which a local commander acts without central authorization.
- An aircraft shootdown or another opening family.

These alternatives were not removed from the arena. They remain separate Crisis Setup families because different true intent and follow-on branches create different Setup identities rather than different World Seeds. The limited fait accompli was selected first because it creates concrete territorial state while exercising the broadest U.S. intelligence, diplomatic, economic, defense, authority, escalation, and adaptation work.

## Consequences

DATE supports the border tension, remote incursions, high-altitude terrain, resource and transport interests, nuclear dyad, and U.S.-Himaldesh relationship; the particular ridge, seizure, intent, and event graph are WOPR-authored. The exact geography, forces, timing, Road to War, STARTEX evidence, MSEL, Open Action Proposal envelope, adjudication boundary, consequences, and probes remain open. A World Seed may vary bounded friction but cannot change Olvana's authorization or limited territorial objective.
