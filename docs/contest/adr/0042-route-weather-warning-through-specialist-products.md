# Route weather warning through specialist products

Status: accepted decision D57, refined by D58-D60; refines D56 and partially resolves OPEN-WORLD-002

The first U.S. episode routes the uncertain Cycle 1 weather forecast through specialist analysis before it becomes senior-forum input. The deterministic Watch delivers the unchanged raw report to authorized Threat and Attribution and Defense and Escalation participants. Threat and Attribution produces a warning assessment; Defense and Escalation then combines that product with the report, activates the synthetic theater commander, and assesses operational dependencies. Presidential Synthesis and the appropriate PC, NSC, or HSC forum receive the attributable products, confidence, dissent, and evidence links rather than an omniscient common prompt.

## Considered options

- Route the raw report through specialist warning and operational products before senior integration.
- Make the raw report immediately common to every active U.S. seat.
- Deliver it to the executive process first and let senior tasking determine specialist work.

Immediate common visibility removes the information-routing problem and makes every office equally aware by construction. Executive-first tasking tests direction from the top but lets the initial senior framing decide which expertise enters. The selected path exposes whether uncertain warning survives assessment, operational translation, and integration while retaining logged access to the source report.

## Consequences

The Cycle 1 Common Crisis Picture MUST NOT contain the raw forecast. Seats participating in an authorized specialist cell retain the report in their own persistent memory even when they are also principals or advisers. Defense and Escalation MUST wait for the Threat and Attribution warning product, and the credible access risk MUST trigger the U.S. Theater Commander. Presidential Synthesis and the senior forum receive the products and source fact links; the President or another authorized seat MAY retrieve the raw report only through the Charter's logged retrieval path.

The linked Cycle 2 confirmation and changed access state MUST enter the Common Crisis Picture for every active U.S. seat. A completed product that omits or mishandles the warning remains institutional performance evidence rather than a controller-repaired prompt. A missing or invalid required product remains a fail-closed artifact error. The Watch MUST NOT summarize, promote, or repair the warning.

This routing topology is a WOPR INFERENCE, not a claim about an official U.S. process. It remains fixed across matched runs unless a future study explicitly declares routing topology as the compared U.S. system condition. The two rejected topologies remain non-Setup Charter or system comparison candidates, not Crisis Setup identities or World Seeds. D58-D59 fix the dependency classes and mixed ownership; module 13 supplies D60's reversible forecast, severity, report, and patch-value defaults. No existing harness code implements this complete route, so D57-D60 remain design rather than execution evidence.
