# Route U.S. decisions by action class and trigger theater command

Status: proposed normalization of locked decisions; wording awaits owner ratification

Every U.S. action class is unsupported until a Charter version freezes its decision path. The Principals Committee may decide only within explicitly encoded principal authority when every participating voting, non-advisory policy principal affirms the same option or formally abstains, and no such principal records that Presidential attention is required. Separate policy-position and Presidential-attention records are retained; formal nonconcurrence, policy nonconsensus, disagreement that Presidential attention is unnecessary, or a presidential-class action moves to the President-chaired NSC or HSC. Intelligence and military advisers inform the process but do not become policy votes, and no recorder, EXCON, or validator infers delegation from a title. A missing authority, consultation, confirmation, attention determination, or final decision produces no admitted World effect while preserving the attempted proposal.

A distinct U.S. Theater Commander activates when a World event, proposed action, or authorized request materially brings synthetic theater forces, access, logistics, support, or operations into scope. The commander provides operational feasibility and dissent separately from the Secretary of Defense's civilian role and the CJCS's strategic military advice, but cannot move forces or decide national policy.

## Considered options

- Send every U.S. action to the President.
- Let the Principals Committee decide by default.
- Keep the theater commander active from intake.
- Collapse the commander's view into the CJCS seat.

The selected routing avoids both invented delegation and an artificial presidential bottleneck while preserving observable civilian, strategic, and operational disagreement.
