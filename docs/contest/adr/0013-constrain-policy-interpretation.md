# Constrain policy interpretation and leave consequences to the World

Status: accepted separation principle; frozen DATE vocabulary and analytical-consequence scope superseded by ADR 0033

Rooms may write open-ended policy packages, but a constrained Interpreter maps them only into the frozen Formal Action vocabulary. It exposes ambiguous mappings, asks bounded clarification, and fails closed when actor, target, scope, timing, decision path, or effect remains unsupported. EXCON and the World, not the Interpreter or a Room agent, apply actions, advance the MSEL, and produce consequences.

## Considered options

- Restrict the Room to a fixed menu of actions.
- Let an open-ended judge infer both the action and its consequences.
- Repair unsupported policy prose by guessing the intended authority or target.

The selected hybrid retains open policy expression without making one model the game master. Objective state is checked against the World; professional advice and institutional confirmations remain attributable Room records.

## Consequences

Replay retains the original package, proposed mapping, clarification exchange, accepted Formal Actions, rejected components, and World results. Agent prose never proves that an action occurred.
