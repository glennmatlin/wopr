# One packet, Nuclear War as World

Status: accepted historical decision; superseded by the locked DATE pivot recorded in draft ADR-0010

The $25k ChinaTalk submission is a single Packet. The object of evaluation is the Room. Nuclear War remains the World. A live human is allowed only as a Demo trajectory, not as the study unit. A second World, a second $25k form, and a live human executive are rejected for this deadline.

Considered options were two $25k entries, a $25k packet plus a $50k concept note, replacing Nuclear War with an EXCOMM or 13 Days world, and putting a human in the study loop. Two entries split fifteen days and weaken the concrete-material claim. A new World cannot meet the replay contract by September 1. A live human breaks matched seeds and receipt comparison.

Consequence: thicken Room prompts and measures on the existing engine. Portability and human-in-the-loop remain prize or later work.
