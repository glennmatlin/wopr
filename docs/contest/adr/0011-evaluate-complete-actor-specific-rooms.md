# Evaluate complete actor-specific Rooms

Status: proposed normalization of locked decisions; wording awaits owner ratification

The reusable evaluation unit is the complete machine-operated national-security Room, not one answer, one chair, or a three-member vote. Each represented actor receives its own source-grounded Room Charter, group graph, seat registry, mandates, information paths, and decision procedure. Matching runs does not require equal seat counts or symmetric institutions.

## Considered options

- Retain the inherited three-agent council for every actor.
- Force all actors into one U.S.-style cabinet.
- Let a setup or model invent the group structure at runtime.

All three were rejected because they would make institutional differences cosmetic or allow the evaluated system to redesign its own test. Seats share their actor's objective vector; mandate, persona, and decision responsibility remain separate, and seats receive no artificial private victory conditions.

## Consequences

Every judgment-bearing office is a persistent seat when activated. Clerical transport, scheduling, recordkeeping, validation, and World execution remain deterministic unless a later source-backed Charter decision assigns them independent judgment.
