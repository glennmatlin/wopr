# Separate the World, authored Crisis Setups, and World Seeds

Status: proposed normalization of locked decisions; wording awaits owner ratification

The Himaldesh-Olvana World owns stable actors, geography, relationships, capabilities, doctrine, formal actions, state transitions, and consequences. A frozen registry of authored Crisis Setups selects a Road to War, STARTEX state, strategic intent, opening incident, private briefs, constraints, and MSEL graph. A World Seed varies bounded uncertainty and friction inside one Setup; it never changes doctrine, political objectives, or whether an incident was accidental or deliberate.

## Considered options

- Fix one illustrative crisis as the whole arena.
- Let a random event deck or seed choose strategic intent.
- Start every Setup with an immediate nuclear-use decision.

These were rejected because they confuse scenario identity with stochastic variation and make one conversation example define the product. Setups begin with conventional border crises; nuclear warning, coercion, misinterpretation, or use remains mechanically reachable when the authored state and actions support it.

## Consequences

Evaluation draws from a versioned Setup registry, while demos may select one trajectory for explanation. MSEL events are authored and causal rather than invented by a policy model or unconstrained judge.
