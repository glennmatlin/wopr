# Pivot the active Packet to a DATE Situation Room arena

Status: accepted historical pivot; single-World scope superseded by ADR 0033

The contest remains one Packet, but its active evaluated object is a machine-operated Situation Room in a new DATE-backed Himaldesh-Olvana World. Nuclear War remains engineering provenance and receipt-bound evidence; it is not the contest-facing realism claim. This supersedes ADRs 0001 through 0008 as the active design while preserving them and ADR 0009 as the record of what actually ran.

## Considered options

- Keep Nuclear War as the submitted World and thicken only its Room prompts.
- Replace it with one fixed historical vignette.
- Build a DATE-derived arena with the actual United States as a friendly external actor and fictional Himaldesh and Olvana as the regional crisis actors.

The DATE arena was selected because the card mechanics did not fit the national-security task, while DATE supplies recognizable exercise design, actor relationships, and a native high-altitude nuclear dyad without forcing a real country into the adversary role.

## Consequences

The current Nuclear War Packet files are historical until they are deliberately rewritten against the consolidated specification. The DATE World must earn its own runnable receipts; old Nuclear War results cannot be represented as evidence that the new arena already runs.
