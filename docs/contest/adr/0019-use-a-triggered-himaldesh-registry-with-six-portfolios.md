# Use a triggered Himaldesh registry with six Cabinet portfolios

Status: proposed normalization of locked decisions; wording awaits owner ratification

The Himaldesh Charter contains every separate judgment-bearing seat supported by the public DATE record, but each Crisis Setup activates only the relevant subset. The Prime Minister, President, Minister of Defense, and General Staff are structural participants. Setup facts, World events, typed candidate actions, and validated consultation requests may activate other registered seats, which then persist for the remainder of the Setup.

The Cabinet registry contains six distinct portfolios: External Affairs; Interior and Border Security; Finance and Economic Resilience; Information and Communications; Civil Infrastructure and Continuity; and Humanitarian and Social Cohesion. Infrastructure and humanitarian work remain separate because physical continuity and distribution of civilian harm can conflict on the same policy.

## Considered options

- Keep a fixed always-active roster.
- Instantiate all 18 referenced ministries as generic agents.
- Let Setups or models invent missing experts.
- Merge infrastructure and humanitarian work solely to reduce the seat count.

The selected six-seat boundary passed bounded Concordia entity-count, isolation, memory, and deterministic-order probes at the reusable seam. It is not an end-to-end Room receipt. A five-portfolio Civil Resilience fallback is authorized only if the first offline tracer bullet demonstrates an intrinsic six-seat feasibility failure, not merely because the legacy C2 artifact expects three members.

DATE supports the represented functions, not the exact six labels, activation predicates, or machine graph. Those are WOPR inferences, and claim-level source-card bindings remain required before Charter ratification.
