# Degrade observation and logistics windows together

Status: accepted decision D58, refined by D59-D60; refines D55-D57 and partially resolves OPEN-WORLD-002

The first High-Altitude Access Degradation directly narrows two distinct predeclared World Affordances: one observation or ISR window and one logistics or support window. They share one exogenous weather event, one event thread, one confirmation, and one atomic State Patch, but each retains its own identity, before-and-after state, dependency links, and downstream consequences. This makes the short episode test both information loss and support feasibility without replacing either with a generic access penalty.

## Considered options

- Degrade both observation or ISR and logistics or support windows.
- Degrade only the logistics or support window.
- Degrade only the observation or ISR window.

Logistics-only pressure is simpler but exercises less of Threat and Attribution. Observation-only pressure gives a clean intelligence problem but weakens the connection to the partner support request and Defense and Escalation. The paired bundle requires the U.S. Room to integrate two professional mandates while remaining small enough for the episode-bounded Core.

## Consequences

The forecast and confirmation MUST identify both dependency classes without prescribing a response. The direct State Patch MUST apply one declared operation to each window against the same base Core version and MUST admit or reject atomically. A one-window update is not a degraded form of the selected inject; it is an invalid direct patch. The two windows MUST remain separately inspectable and MUST NOT collapse into one scalar access score.

The matched direct event and both writes remain identical across normal runs. Cycle 1 policy MAY establish alternatives, reduce exposure, change commitments, or leave either window unused, so state-dependent descendants MAY diverge after the common patch. Neither direct write may reveal Olvanan intent, create a capability, force U.S. involvement, or determine withdrawal or recapture.

Observation-only and logistics-only bundles remain separately authorable Crisis Setup identities, not World Seeds or U.S. system conditions. D59 selects one U.S.-enabled observation window and one Himaldeshi-controlled logistics window for the first Setup; all-Himaldeshi and all-U.S.-enabled ownership profiles remain future Setup identities. Exact synthetic names, values, duration, recovery, report bytes, and patch serialization are reversible Authoring Defaults under D60. No existing DATE implementation applies or replays this paired mixed-ownership patch, so D58-D60 remain design rather than execution evidence.
