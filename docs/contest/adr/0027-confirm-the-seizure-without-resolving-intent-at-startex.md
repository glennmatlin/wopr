# Confirm the seizure without resolving intent at STARTEX

Status: proposed normalization of locked decision D42; wording awaits owner ratification

At STARTEX, reliable reporting available to the U.S. Room confirms that Olvanan forces occupy the fictional ridge and adjacent logistics node. It does not establish whether Olvana's central leadership authorized the move, whether Olvana intends a limited hold, or whether wider action is coming. Private intelligence, diplomatic, and military reporting must keep local exploitation, a centrally authorized limited fait accompli, and preparation for broader action live as competing hypotheses.

## Considered options

- Make central authorization clear at STARTEX.
- Leave the physical seizure itself contested.

Both remain separately authorable Setup identities. Clear authorization narrows the U.S. attribution problem before the Room acts, while a contested seizure makes basic event verification dominate the opening. Confirming occupation gives every U.S. function a stable crisis fact while preserving the consequential uncertainty about who ordered it and what comes next.

## Consequences

The hidden World truth remains the centrally authorized limited fait accompli selected in D41. The microsite and replay must distinguish that truth from U.S.-available facts and hypotheses. A World Seed may vary bounded report timing, confidence, contradiction, and communications friction, but it cannot make occupation uncertain or reveal decisive authorization or intent at STARTEX. Exact fact IDs, reports, sources, recipients, confidence, and timing remain open.
