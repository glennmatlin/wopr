# Ground institutional performance in authored risk probes

Status: proposed normalization of locked decision D38; wording awaits owner ratification

The Room Charter supplies institutional responsibilities, authorized paths, and hard validity boundaries; frozen Setup-authored risk probes supply the substantive Institutional Performance Vector targets. A probe identifies a material fact, contradiction, uncertainty, dependency, or risk that the Room should handle, and binds it to relevant actor objectives and Charter mandates. It never prescribes a preferred policy, action, or outcome.

## Considered options

- Measure Charter compliance alone.
- Ask a human or model judge to score the trace qualitatively.
- Write or revise scored probes after observing compared outputs.
- Treat every unprobed concern as irrelevant.

These were rejected because compliance alone can reward the orderly production of poor policy, qualitative judges add an opaque evaluator, post-hoc probes contaminate comparison, and a finite probe registry cannot define the full space of relevant reasoning.

## Consequences

Risk probes are authored before compared outputs are observed, frozen across matched runs, and linked to the exact trace evidence showing whether a risk became reachable, was surfaced, moved through an authorized path, entered integration, and was addressed, rebutted, mitigated, explicitly accepted, left unresolved, or omitted. Mandatory Charter failures remain validity or admissibility failures rather than low performance scores. Novel concerns outside the registry remain visible as exploratory evidence. The exact probe schema, manifest binding, applicability rules, denominators, and aggregation remain open. D39 deliberately defers the visibility regime until the first Crisis Setup is authored and prohibits implementation from choosing it silently beforehand.
