# Use Prime Minister drafting, targeted review, and visible dissent

Status: proposed normalization of locked decisions; wording awaits owner ratification

After portfolio products and evidence requests are complete, the Prime Minister authors a versioned Cabinet Policy Draft. A deterministic Charter router selects every affected portfolio; the Prime Minister cannot choose the review panel. Independent reviewers inspect the same frozen draft in parallel, results are collected in Charter order, and every material revision is rerouted to the portfolios affected by the changed components.

Portfolio support or opposition remains advisory unless a separate frozen Required Confirmation applies. The Prime Minister may proceed over advisory opposition only with an attributable Dissent Disposition that records adoption, mitigation, narrowing, withdrawal, or an explicit unresolved risk and stop condition. The original review is never rewritten as concurrence.

## Considered options

- Run all-portfolio bargaining before the Prime Minister drafts.
- Let a deterministic secretariat author the policy.
- Aggregate reviews by majority or weights.
- Give every portfolio a general veto.

These were rejected because they displace the Prime Minister's Cabinet role or turn professional review into another generic council. Missing reviews and missing confirmations remain distinct fail-closed states.

The drafting, routing, review, and dissent procedure is a WOPR design inference from DATE-supported offices. It is not a published Himaldeshi Cabinet procedure, and its source-card bindings remain incomplete.
