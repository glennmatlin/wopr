# Split Himaldesh executive authority and cell work

Status: proposed normalization of locked decisions; wording awaits owner ratification

Himaldesh has two persistent executive centers. The Prime Minister leads civilian national-security policy and Cabinet integration; the President acts as Supreme Commander for military decisions and provides the distinct presidential confirmation required for strategic-force action. Civilian and military instruments that cross both portfolios require both offices, while neither office may silently absorb the other's published function.

A Prime Minister-led Cabinet Policy Cell and President-led Command Cell develop independent products concurrently when their inputs and active seats are disjoint. The Minister of Defense is one persistent bridge seat, not one clone in each cell. A Joint Executive Session occurs only when the typed action class requires both executives.

## Considered options

- Make the President relevant only to strategic use.
- Put every cycle under a President-led command authority.
- Use one generic executive agent or standing co-chaired forum.
- Run Cabinet work first and military review only after policy converges.

These were rejected because DATE divides overall national-security responsibility, supreme command, defense management, and strategic authority across distinct institutions. The exact machine procedure is a WOPR inference and must not be represented as an official Himaldeshi constitutional process.

Claim-level source cards for the DATE facts and their mapping to each inferred decision path remain required before Charter ratification.
