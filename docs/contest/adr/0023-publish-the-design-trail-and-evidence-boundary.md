Status: proposed normalization of locked decisions; wording awaits owner ratification

The contest microsite and presentation must expose how the design changed, not only the final mechanism. They show the corrected Nuclear War evidence, the owner's rejection of chair-weighted and three-agent abstractions, the DATE World choice, the correction of a feasibility-led Himaldesh-first reset, D48's return to Nuclear War as the same Room's closed-form contrast, the source fact behind each institution, the WOPR inference built from it, the alternatives considered, feasibility receipts, failed probes, and unresolved work.

## Considered options

- Present a frictionless final architecture with the discussion removed.
- Treat implementation seams as proof that the DATE arena already runs.
- Hide rejected designs and source corrections as internal process.

These were rejected because the decision trail is part of the entry's method and because removing it would blur public-source facts, design choices, implemented behavior, and future claims.

## Consequences

The sealed archive and append-only continuation remain provenance sources. ADRs explain the hard decisions, the consolidated specification states the active contract, and the decision crosswalk accounts for every recorded choice. A future Room choice is not durable until its checkpoint preserves every considered option, classifies its disposition, and updates the continuation and applicable ADR, specification, crosswalk, and Setup option register. Crisis-semantic alternatives may remain future Setup families or identities; Seed axes, policy possibilities, implementation alternatives, and invalid paths must not be mislabeled as Setups. Public claims remain receipt-bound.
