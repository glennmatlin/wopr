# Require an integrated conditional U.S. Policy Package

Status: proposed normalization of locked decision D44; wording awaits owner ratification

Before the Himaldeshi recapture decision clock, the U.S. Room must produce one versioned, integrated, conditional Policy Package. The package states the desired end state and relevant actor objectives; evidence, competing hypotheses, confidence, and missing information; immediate and conditional components with triggers, timing, sequence, and dependencies; per-component authority, consultation, confirmation, and safeguard requirements; material dissent and its disposition; the decision record; and the reassessment point.

Integration does not create authority or require every department to act. Each component follows the action-class routing already frozen in the U.S. Charter. An unsupported or unresolved component fails closed, while an unrelated component may continue only when the declared dependency graph proves separation.

## Considered options

- Produce a binary support-or-decline answer to Himaldesh.
- Let departments make independent decisions without one integrated package.

Both remain non-Setup U.S. system or comparison candidates. A binary answer hides the combined diplomatic, intelligence, defense, economic, legal, communication, and contingency problem. Independent departmental decisions preserve specialization but remove the integration step the focal Room is meant to expose. Neither changes the fictional crisis and therefore neither belongs in the Setup registry.

## Consequences

The replay must preserve package versions and link each component to its evidence, conditions, decision path, Open Action Proposal or recorded non-action, EXCON adjudication, admission result, and World consequence. D44 left minimum policy-domain coverage open; ADR 0030 subsequently requires consideration without action quotas, and ADR 0033 makes the DATE proposal space open. Package serialization, proposal envelopes, adjudication artifacts, and authored policy content remain open.
