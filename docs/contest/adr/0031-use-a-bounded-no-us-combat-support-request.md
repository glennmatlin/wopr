# Use a bounded no-U.S.-combat support request

Status: proposed normalization of locked decision D46; wording awaits owner ratification

The first Crisis Setup must give the U.S. Room a concrete multidomain request from Himaldesh: urgent consultation; private and public diplomatic pressure for Olvanan withdrawal; time-sensitive intelligence sharing; planning, logistics, defensive materiel, and force-protection support for Himaldesh's own limited recapture; and coordination on targeted economic pressure if Olvana does not withdraw. The request explicitly excludes U.S. target nomination or selection, fires, combat forces, and any invented treaty guarantee.

The bundle creates decision pressure without deciding the U.S. response or Himaldesh's later recapture authorization. The U.S. may support, narrow, condition, defer, or decline every requested item through the D44-D45 package and existing action-class routes.

## Considered options

- Use a narrow request limited to diplomatic backing and intelligence sharing.
- Request direct U.S. operational support, including targeting, air support, fires, or forces.

Both remain future Setup-identity candidates. The narrow bundle exercises less of the complete U.S. Room. The direct operational bundle centers the episode on intervention and immediate presidential decisions. Neither may be selected by World Seed because the request changes the authored political pressure.

## Consequences

The exact request wording, delivery channel, public and private posture, clock, withdrawal predicate, and Himaldeshi decision mechanics remain open. The selected categories and exclusions must be frozen and identical across matched runs.
