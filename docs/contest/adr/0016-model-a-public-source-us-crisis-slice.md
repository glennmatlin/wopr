# Model a dated public-source U.S. crisis slice

Status: accepted as amended by ADR 0033

The U.S. Room is a dated public-source abstraction with a stable institutional core and a versioned `US_PUBLIC_2026Q3` posture. It records the complete public membership, adviser, invitee, and committee classes, then activates only the offices relevant to the current issue. Every relevant principal or adviser is a distinct persistent agent when activated; deputies and staff ranks are represented inside office and cell products rather than duplicated as first-slice agents.

The Room has four operational layers: deterministic Watch and communications, activated specialist cells, National Security Advisor-chaired integration with presidential forums when required, and deterministic recording and state admission. Under D48, a distinct creative EXCON may propose DATE consequences between the Room decision and deterministic World validation.

Public membership and statutory office boundaries are source facts. The objective vector, synthetic entitlements, first-slice activation, cell graph, and machine procedure are WOPR design inferences whose claim-level source cards and remaining Charter fields must be completed before ratification.

## Considered options

- Reduce the U.S. Room to three generic senior advisers.
- Instantiate every public staff rank in every cycle.
- Give the President every private brief automatically.

These were rejected because the first erases professional conflict, the second duplicates ranks and turns, and the third bypasses the institution's information work. The U.S. Charter names no current officeholder and claims neither classified fidelity nor official endorsement.
