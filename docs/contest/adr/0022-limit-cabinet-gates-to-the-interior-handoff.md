# Limit initial Cabinet gates to the Interior force handoff

Status: proposed normalization of locked decisions; wording awaits owner ratification

A Cabinet portfolio receives a binding Required Confirmation only for a frozen, source-backed, typed action dependency with a bounded question that cannot be replaced by a World fact or deterministic validation. There is no gate quota and no symmetry requirement. The initial map contains one rule: transfer of Interior-administered forces to Defense operational control requires Interior to confirm that the administrative handoff, continuing domestic coverage, and reversion arrangement are executable.

The rule does not give Interior a veto over the military objective or national policy. The World owns force identity, location, readiness, equipment, current assignment, legal availability encoded by the Setup, transfer execution, and consequences. A missing or negative Interior result blocks only the transfer component and its declared dependents. External Affairs, Finance, Information, Civil Infrastructure, and Humanitarian portfolios have no current binding gate; a later typed action must independently pass the same eligibility test.

## Considered options

- Give every portfolio at least one gate.
- Leave all Cabinet portfolios advisory forever.
- Let a reviewing portfolio declare its own prerequisite.
- Ask a model to confirm objective World state.

These were rejected because they either invent authority, forbid a real institutional dependency, or replace reproducible state checks with probabilistic assertions.

DATE supplies the Interior-to-Defense administrative relationship. The action class, bounded confirmation question, condition semantics, and failure rule are WOPR inferences whose claim-level source-card binding remains required before Charter ratification.
