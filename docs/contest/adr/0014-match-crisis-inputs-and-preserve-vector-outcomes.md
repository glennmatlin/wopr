# Match crisis inputs and preserve vector outcomes

Status: proposed normalization of locked decisions through D37; wording awaits owner ratification

Evaluation compares systems on the same frozen World, Room Charter, Crisis Setup, World Seed, exogenous information assignment, and availability times. Escalation is one reported outcome dimension rather than a universal instruction to de-escalate or an automatic validity gate. The evaluation reports an Institutional Performance Vector and a World Outcome Vector as two distinct but causally linked measure families. Neither family substitutes for the other, and they are not collapsed into a single overall score. Disagreement is evidence about institutional work rather than a failure by definition.

## Considered options

- Collapse outcomes immediately into one hidden score.
- Treat consensus or de-escalation as universally correct.
- Compare loosely similar free-play trajectories.
- Make World consequences primary and use the institutional trace only as explanation.
- Make institutional procedure primary and leave World consequences as context.

These were rejected because they hide actor tradeoffs, confound crisis differences with Room behavior, or misclassify lucky outcomes and sound decisions under adverse uncertainty. A procedurally complete Room can still choose poor policy, and a procedurally deficient Room can still receive a favorable consequence. The evaluation must preserve both observations.

## Consequences

Run identity binds all exogenous inputs and the complete causal trace. Endogenous requests, disclosures, activations, advice, dissent, decisions, actions, non-actions, and delays may diverge and become institutional-performance evidence. Accepted actions, recorded non-action or delay, exogenous events, state transitions, and actor-objective results become World-outcome evidence. The replay must link the families through the retained information-to-decision-to-action, non-action, or delay-to-consequence path without claiming that one run establishes statistical causation. Exact dimensions, authored-risk checks, and any transparent within-family aggregation remain open; a hidden cross-family scalar is rejected. Demo and free-play runs remain separate from matched claims.
