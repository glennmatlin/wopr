# Use a partner request under a recapture decision clock

Status: proposed normalization of locked decision D43; wording awaits owner ratification

The first Crisis Setup must include an urgent Himaldeshi request for U.S. consultation and support. Himaldesh is preparing a limited recapture if Olvana does not withdraw, but its Room has not yet authorized execution and will decide at a frozen MSEL time. The U.S. Room must reach its own response decision before that time; action, conditional action, non-action, and delay remain visible possibilities rather than prescribed answers.

## Considered options

- Have Himaldesh hold fire and request mediation only.
- Have Himaldesh request direct U.S. combat support to retake the position.

Both remain future Setup-identity candidates. A mediation-only posture exercises less of the U.S. defense and authority system, while a direct combat request narrows the first episode too quickly around intervention. The selected pressure activates the full U.S. institution while leaving policy and Himaldesh's later decision open.

## Consequences

D43 left the exact request and support categories open; ADR 0031 subsequently fixes a bounded no-U.S.-combat bundle while leaving exact wording, delivery, deadline, withdrawal predicate, public posture, and Himaldeshi decision mechanics open. The request category and recapture posture belong to Setup identity rather than World Seed; matched runs must freeze the exact clock and information. The World records U.S. non-action or delay and advances the same authored deadline without treating either as an invalid run.
