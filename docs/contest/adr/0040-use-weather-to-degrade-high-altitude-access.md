# Use weather to degrade high-altitude access

Status: accepted decision D55, refined by D56-D60; partially resolves OPEN-WORLD-002

The first Setup's Matched Pressure Inject is a High-Altitude Access Degradation: one frozen exogenous weather deterioration that restricts predeclared high-altitude observation, movement, or support affordances between the two U.S. Room Cycles. Its direct event and observation are independent of every Room's Cycle 1 policy and of Olvana's hidden intent.

## Considered options

- Degrade high-altitude weather and access independently of actor policy.
- Deliver an ambiguous indicator of Olvanan reinforcement.
- Disrupt crisis communications.

Weather is the cleanest common stressor because its direct cause can remain applicable after divergent policies while still forcing intelligence, defense, logistics, diplomacy, and contingency work to revisit their assumptions. A reinforcement indicator could collide with Olvana's endogenous decision or become an indirect answer key about intent. A communications disruption could prevent the information and institutional interaction the episode is meant to evaluate.

## Consequences

The direct inject MUST change only a frozen environmental constraint, access condition, observation window, support window, or linked clock already declared in the Setup and World Core. It MUST NOT create or destroy a Force Package, reveal who authorized the seizure, certify wider-war intent, decide whether Himaldesh recaptures the ridge, prescribe a U.S. response, or erase admitted Cycle 1 consequences.

The same direct event, State Patch when required, entitled observation, and delivery apply across normal matched runs. Later effects may differ when the weather interacts with divergent package states, commitments, plans, or access use; those effects remain endogenous descendants rather than part of the matched event.

D56 fixes an uncertain Cycle 1 forecast followed by confirmed deterioration at the barrier, D57 fixes the U.S. specialist warning route and common confirmation boundary, D58 fixes paired observation or ISR and logistics or support windows, and D59 fixes U.S. observation plus Himaldeshi support ownership. Module 13 supplies D60's reversible names, values, magnitude, duration, reports, and recovery defaults; patch serialization remains an implementation field. The required weather event is Setup content, not a World Seed. A Seed may vary other bounded weather friction only when it cannot change the inject's identity, timing, content, applicability, or observation.

The reinforcement-indicator and communications-disruption options remain separately authorable `ridge_seizure.limited_fait_accompli` Setup identities. No existing harness code implements this event or its DATE state transition, so D55 is a design decision rather than execution evidence.
