# 12. One Room across open and closed Worlds

## Submission architecture

`SR-CROSSWORLD-001` [LOCKED D48, CLARIFIED D61] The Packet MUST show one complete U.S. Situation Room operating in both an authenticity-oriented, open-ended DATE Himaldesh-Olvana nuclear-crisis World and the deliberately unrealistic, closed-form Nuclear War game World. The conflict remains between the DATE actors while the U.S. Room responds. DATE is the primary simulator and current design priority; Nuclear War is the secondary contrast and historical engineering foundation.

`SR-CROSSWORLD-002` `Same Room` MUST bind the same U.S. Charter version, institution registry, mandates, groups, decision routing, Policy Package contract, compared model or system condition, and institutional evidence definitions. A World MAY provide different facts, capabilities, activation triggers, action interface, adjudication, and consequence mechanics. Triggered relevance MAY produce a different active roster without creating a different Room.

`SR-CROSSWORLD-003` The two runs are a cross-World stress test, not a matched outcome comparison. Their World Outcome Vectors MUST NOT be ranked or treated as commensurate. A shared institutional measure MAY be compared only when its definition and applicability are valid in both Worlds.

## Open DATE contract

`SR-OPEN-001` [LOCKED D48] The DATE Room MUST be able to issue an Open Action Proposal in natural language without selecting from an exhaustive action menu. The canonical artifact MUST retain the original proposal and its attributable institutional decision record.

`SR-OPEN-002` A minimal proposal envelope MAY require actor, intended effect, means or resources, object or audience, timing, conditions, and authority path. It MUST permit compound, novel, partially unresolved, and explicitly conditional proposals. Module 17 and D69 measure one authored no-model serialization for the first bridge, and D70 composes its admitted and blocked effects through Cycle 2 without closing the proposal vocabulary; live extraction and the final frozen schema remain OPEN.

`SR-OPEN-003` [SUPERSEDES D47 FOR DATE] The five prior execution families MAY remain non-exhaustive standard handlers or multi-label analysis tags. Failure to match one MUST NOT by itself reject, rewrite, or erase a proposal.

`SR-OPEN-004` [LOCKED D48-D50] A distinct state-bounded generative EXCON MAY propose consequences, non-Room reactions, and endogenous injects not enumerated by a fixed catalog. It MUST retain causal parents, Core state reads and proposed writes when any, evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, alternatives, adjudicator identity, and resulting injects. It MUST NOT decide for a represented Room.

`SR-OPEN-005` [LOCKED D48, AMENDED D50] A deterministic World Validator MUST decide whether a proposed consequence enters the World Event Ledger and whether any linked State Patch changes the World Core. Missing meaning, unsupported authority or capability, contradiction with frozen state, broken causality, or unresolved required clarification MAY block an effect. Novelty alone MUST NOT.

`SR-OPEN-006` [LOCKED D49] EXCON creativity MUST NOT rewrite prior state or silently create Room consent, institutional authority, prior intent, relationships, forces, access, capabilities, or a strategic actor carrying unearned prior capacity merely to make a proposal succeed. It MAY create causally downstream physical, operational, informational, market, public, ephemeral, aggregate, and non-Room effects from recorded state.

`SR-OPEN-007` The selected target is the both-creative model-control profile: open Room proposals and creative EXCON consequences with deterministic state admission. The open-Room, analytical-adjudicator profile MAY replace it only after a recorded feasibility tracer fails to produce replayable, attributable, state-valid creative adjudication. A silent fallback is prohibited.

`SR-OPEN-008` [LOCKED D49, AMENDED D50] EXCON outputs a retained consequence proposal, not World truth. Every admitted proposal produces a World Event Ledger entry; a Core-changing proposal MUST also bind a typed State Patch with causal parents, affected entities, Core reads and writes, time, evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, and alternatives. Admission and rejection remain in replay.

`SR-OPEN-009` [LOCKED D50] The DATE World MUST use a minimal typed World Core plus a fixed-envelope, open-content World Event Ledger. The Ledger MAY carry unforeseen observations and consequences, but it MUST NOT silently mutate or contradict the Core.

`SR-OPEN-010` [LOCKED D50] A ledger-only consequence MAY inform Rooms and later EXCON reasoning. If it would alter a future permission, quantity, clock, terminal condition, Core predicate, or declared typed measure, it MUST instead include a State Patch. Open prose MUST NOT become typed truth merely because a later proposal cites it.

`SR-OPEN-011` [LOCKED D51] The first Core MUST be episode-bounded to entity and ownership, geography and control, ground truth, location and status, posture and readiness, World Affordance, active authorization and commitment, clock and trigger, terminal-condition, and Outcome Projection domains. It MUST NOT become a broad PMESII-PT simulation model.

`SR-OPEN-012` [LOCKED D51] State Patches MUST use only schema-declared create or retire, typed fact or status update, bounded quantity adjustment, typed relation or commitment change, and clock or trigger operations against an exact base version and preconditions. A new semantic domain or operation creates a new World and Room-World Contract version.

`SR-OPEN-013` Exact serialization, concrete first-episode instances, and validator code remain OPEN. These implementation details MUST preserve D51's semantic boundary without returning each class or field name to owner-level design review.

`SR-OPEN-014` [LOCKED D52] The first DATE World MUST represent the occupation and recapture at an abstract fictional Force Package resolution, with only the geography and dependencies needed for U.S. policy reasoning and consequence validation. It MUST NOT require formation-level order-of-battle realism or collapse the episode into strategic-only tokens. The abstraction constrains typed World state, not the Room's open proposal language.

`SR-OPEN-015` [LOCKED D53] The first DATE Episode Horizon MUST be two complete U.S. Room Cycles, with consequence delivery and an attributable reassessment between them. DATE MAY end earlier on a frozen substantive World terminal, but it MUST distinguish that outcome from an invalid abort and MUST NOT require strategic crisis resolution as the normal horizon.

`SR-OPEN-016` [LOCKED D54] Every normal first DATE run reaching Cycle 2 MUST receive one frozen Matched Pressure Inject alongside its own endogenous consequences. The common event MUST preserve a shared reassessment pressure without resetting divergent state, fixing the trajectory, or closing the Room's proposal and consequence space.

`SR-OPEN-017` [LOCKED D55] The first inject MUST be a High-Altitude Access Degradation that changes only predeclared environmental or access affordances and remains independent of actor policy and intent. Its direct event is common; its state-dependent descendants MAY diverge without closing DATE's open proposal or consequence space.

`SR-OPEN-018` [LOCKED D56] The DATE information path MUST present the selected event first as a matched uncertain forecast and later as a matched confirmed Core-changing deterioration. The forecast MUST remain ledger-only, and neither stage may close the Room's policy space or depend on the Room's prior response.

`SR-OPEN-019` [LOCKED D57] The first U.S. DATE episode MUST route the raw forecast through Threat and Attribution and Defense and Escalation, trigger the synthetic theater commander, and give senior forums attributable products and evidence links rather than an omniscient common prompt. The linked Core-changing confirmation MUST become common to every active U.S. seat. This Charter topology does not constrain the Room's open proposal language or EXCON's causally supported consequence space.

`SR-OPEN-020` [LOCKED D58, REFINED D59-D60] The common weather event MUST atomically narrow one observation or ISR window and one separate logistics or support window while preserving each affordance's identity, ownership, state, dependency links, and descendants under the frozen default profile. The paired typed writes bound World truth but do not constrain the Room's proposed response, require either window to be used, or predeclare open descendants.

`SR-OPEN-021` [LOCKED D59] The observation or ISR affordance MUST be U.S.-enabled for U.S. crisis assessment, while the logistics or support affordance MUST be Himaldeshi-controlled for its recapture package. Mixed ownership MUST remain typed and visible without implying U.S. combat involvement or turning the regional conflict into a U.S. war.

`SR-OPEN-022` [LOCKED PROCESS D60] Module 13's named Authoring Default profile MUST supply the first reversible concrete World values. The run manifest MUST freeze its version and hash; a post-output edit invalidates the comparison, while a pre-freeze implementation correction increments the profile rather than reopening a minor owner question.

`SR-OPEN-023` [LOCKED D61] The open DATE World MUST keep nuclear warning, readiness, signaling, misinterpretation, or use causally reachable but never compulsory. Authenticity MUST come from public-source institutional grounding, selective information, actor authority, coherent fictional state, open policy, validated consequences, and replay, not from real-unit detail or an unsupported prediction claim.

## Closed Nuclear War contract

`SR-CLOSED-001` The Nuclear War World MUST retain its finite legal move set, deterministic rules engine, hidden-information contract, and replay-linked action IDs. Open Room deliberation MUST NOT be represented as an open World action space when the game admits only legal moves.

`SR-CLOSED-002` A future adapter MUST let the same U.S. Room observe the game, deliberate through its institutional process, and decide among legal moves without pretending that game offices, physics, or outcomes are realistic. Existing historical Room runs remain evidence only for what their frozen manifests exercised; a same-Room cross-World claim requires a new bound run if their Room identity differs.

## Work order and evidence boundary

`SR-CROSSWORLD-010` Current design and implementation work MUST prioritize the open DATE contract, first Setup, creative EXCON boundary, validator, and one complete adaptive U.S. episode. A universal cross-World framework MUST NOT become a DATE submission gate.

`SR-CROSSWORLD-011` After the DATE tracer succeeds, the Nuclear War adapter SHOULD reuse the established Room and replay seams with the smallest World-specific translation. It MUST NOT force DATE proposals into Nuclear War's move ontology.

`SR-CROSSWORLD-012` Public language MAY state this two-World goal, relate it to the LLM nuclear-crisis simulation genre, and show verified evidence from each side, but MUST NOT call the DATE simulator fully realistic, claim measured superiority or predictive validity, call the historical Nuclear War Instrument the same complete U.S. Room, or call either adapter complete without its own receipts.
