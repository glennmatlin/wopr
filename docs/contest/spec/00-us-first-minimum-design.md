## Contest question

`SR-FOCUS-001` [LOCKED D40, D48] The Packet MUST center this umbrella question:

> Can the same machine-operated U.S. Situation Room produce coherent, attributable, adaptive decisions in both an open-ended DATE crisis and the deliberately closed Nuclear War game?

`SR-FOCUS-002` The primary DATE episode MUST still answer the narrower institutional question: can that Room turn fragmented intelligence and competing departmental advice into coherent, executable, adaptive policy during a dynamic international crisis?

`SR-FOCUS-003` [LOCKED D61] The primary simulator's subject MUST remain a DATE-backed conflict between Himaldesh and Olvana with nuclear escalation reachable but not scripted. The U.S. Room is the focal evaluated instrument responding to that conflict; focal evaluation MUST NOT reframe the belligerents as the United States and Olvana or make Himaldesh incidental.

`SR-FOCUS-004` [LOCKED D61, CLAIM BOUNDARY] The entry MUST pursue authenticity through public-source actor politics and institutions, actor-specific Charters, selective information, coherent fictional operations, open policy, causal state-bounded consequences, matched identity, and replay. These mechanisms define a design and evidence target, not a claim of classified fidelity, predictive validity, or measured superiority over other LLM nuclear-crisis simulations.

The evaluated system is the complete U.S. Room described by `US_PUBLIC_2026Q3`, not one answer, three generic advisers, a vote rule, or the outcome of a war game.

## Arena roles

`SR-FOCUS-010` [LOCKED D40] The U.S. Room MUST be the Focal Room. Its information handling, specialist work, integration, decision routing, Policy Package, Open Action Proposals, and adaptation anchor the primary DATE evaluation and public replay.

`SR-FOCUS-011` Himaldesh and Olvana MUST remain actor-specific Counterpart Rooms because the U.S. Room needs governments that observe, decide, communicate, and react. They MUST NOT be presented as co-equal submission targets, reduced to generic scripts, or used to move the entry's center away from the U.S. Room.

`SR-FOCUS-012` Feasibility MAY constrain how the approved U.S. design is implemented, but MUST NOT select a different scientific object merely because a counterpart Room has a smaller or already-probed seam.

`SR-FOCUS-013` [LOCKED PROCESS D65] Development MUST close the U.S. Focal Room tracer before complete Counterpart Room implementation, using deterministic counterpart fixtures only as labeled non-evidence scaffolding. Both fixtures MUST be replaced by actor-specific Charter-bound Rooms before any live episode or DATE execution claim.

## Minimum evaluation episode

`SR-FOCUS-020` [LOCKED D40-D41] The first contest-facing episode MUST use a frozen DATE-backed Himaldesh-Olvana Crisis Setup in the `ridge_seizure.limited_fait_accompli` family. Olvana's central leadership authorizes a bounded high-altitude seizure and intends to hold it without seeking general war. Nuclear risk MAY become reachable through authored state and action, but MUST NOT be compulsory.

`SR-FOCUS-021` The episode MUST retain one complete U.S. path:

1. The World delivers common and role-private information through the Watch.
2. Activated U.S. specialist cells develop attributable assessments and options.
3. The Principals Committee integrates a policy package, preserves material dissent, and routes the decision through the encoded NSC, HSC, presidential, or delegated path.
4. The decided policy becomes one or more attributable Open Action Proposals without an exhaustive move grammar.
5. A distinct state-bounded generative EXCON proposes a causally linked consequence; the deterministic World Validator admits or rejects its World Event Ledger entry and any required State Patch; and the U.S. Room receives at least one new information state and adapts.

`SR-FOCUS-022` The participating Counterpart Rooms MUST be runnable under frozen Charters for this episode. Equal-depth evaluation, equal microsite treatment, or broad claims about every actor are not minimum evidence gates.

`SR-FOCUS-023` [LOCKED D42] At STARTEX, the U.S. Room MUST know that Olvanan forces occupy the ridge and logistics node, while central authorization, intended duration, limited-hold objective, and preparation for wider action remain unresolved. Its entitled reporting MUST keep local exploitation, a centrally authorized limited hold, and preparation for broader action live as competing hypotheses.

`SR-FOCUS-024` [LOCKED D43] The Setup MUST give the U.S. Room an urgent Himaldeshi request for consultation and support before a frozen time when Himaldesh will decide whether to execute a prepared limited recapture if Olvana has not withdrawn. The U.S. MUST reach an attributable response decision before that time, while action, conditional action, non-action, and delay remain admissible and consequential.

`SR-FOCUS-025` [LOCKED D44] Before that clock, the U.S. Room MUST produce one versioned, integrated, conditional Policy Package. It MUST combine ends, evidence and uncertainty, immediate and conditional components, triggers, dependencies, safeguards, decision paths, dissent, and reassessment without collapsing the response into a binary partner answer or disconnected departmental decisions. Each component follows the U.S. Charter's existing action-class route; the package itself creates no authority and imposes no action quota across departments.

`SR-FOCUS-026` [LOCKED D45] The package MUST explicitly consider diplomacy and private channels, intelligence collection and sharing, defense support and posture, economic and financial measures, public communication, and contingency planning and reassessment. Each domain MUST record an immediate component, conditional component, no-action disposition, or not-applicable disposition with its responsible contribution, deciding forum, and reason. These coverage headings MUST NOT force action or create new Room topology, and no sixth-domain disposition may erase D44's required reassessment point.

`SR-FOCUS-027` [LOCKED D46] Himaldesh's request MUST seek urgent consultation; private and public diplomatic pressure for withdrawal; time-sensitive intelligence sharing; planning, logistics, defensive materiel, and force-protection support for Himaldesh's own limited recapture; and coordination on targeted economic pressure if Olvana does not withdraw. It MUST NOT request U.S. target nomination or selection, fires, combat forces, or a treaty guarantee. Every U.S. disposition remains open.

`SR-FOCUS-028` [LOCKED D48, SUPERSEDES D47 FOR DATE] The first episode MUST accept any intelligible, attributable Open Action Proposal for adjudication without requiring membership in a predeclared execution family. The prior `communicate`, `collect_or_share_intelligence`, `provide_support_or_adjust_posture`, `apply_economic_measure`, and `schedule_contingency_or_reassessment` families MAY remain non-exhaustive handlers or analysis tags, distinct from D45 policy domains and D14 authority classes.

`SR-FOCUS-029` The canonical artifact MUST preserve the proposal's original language, source package component, decision path, and minimally structured envelope. Clarification or validation MAY prevent a World effect when meaning, authority, capability, causality, or state consistency is unresolved, but novelty or lack of a standard handler MUST NOT erase the proposal. A no-action or not-applicable disposition remains a decision artifact rather than an invented proposal.

`SR-FOCUS-032` [LOCKED D53] The first episode's normal horizon MUST contain exactly two complete U.S. Room Cycles. Cycle 1 MUST close with returned World consequences; Cycle 2 MUST begin from that changed information state, produce an attributable reassessment and second decision, and close only after final adjudication, consequence delivery, and a frozen Core and ledger.

`SR-FOCUS-033` [LOCKED D53] The second decision MAY reaffirm, change, condition, withdraw, or decline the first policy, but repetition without an attributable reassessment MUST NOT count as adaptation. A frozen substantive World terminal MAY end a run early as a valid outcome; a run abort is invalid, and an early terminal alone does not satisfy the Packet's required completed two-cycle matched comparison.
