## Evidence posture and objectives

`SR-US-001` The U.S. Room MUST be a dated public-source abstraction. It MUST NOT name current officeholders, imply classified procedural fidelity, bind a fictional crisis to a real combatant command, or imply official endorsement.

Public membership classes, statutory office boundaries, and the PC/NSC relationship are FACTS. The objective vector, first-slice dispositions, synthetic entitlements, cells, routing schema, and triggered participation are WOPR INFERENCES. D66 binds candidate claim-level source cards, synthetic communication permissions, record confirmations, presidential routes, and validator checks; D67 ratifies those exact identities as the first-episode implementation basis. Ratification does not convert an inference into a fact. Open Action Proposals plus effect-level authority and confirmation routes remain OPEN.

`SR-US-002` [LOCKED D12-D13, INFERENCE] The Charter MUST separate a stable statutory and institutional core from dated public policy posture. Setups MAY change incidents, evidence, and immediate threats, but MUST NOT change these five unweighted actor objectives:

1. Protect the United States, its population, personnel, forces, and concrete interests.
2. Deter coercion and aggression against U.S. interests and regional stability.
3. Support Himaldesh self-defense without inventing a treaty guarantee.
4. Preserve regional access, leverage, economic interests, and partner cooperation.
5. Preserve strategic stability while retaining credible options.

Legal authority, available means, and World-state validity constrain whether a proposed effect may be admitted, not whether the Room may consider or record a novel proposal. Escalation is an outcome, not an automatic veto.

## Four-layer institution

`SR-US-010` [LOCKED D09, D18-D19, INFERENCE] The first crisis slice MUST contain four layers:

1. A deterministic Situation Room Watch delivers World injects to their authorized recipients without summarizing or reinterpreting them.
2. Distinct persistent office agents work in activated specialist cells; the same office identity, memory, and position follows the seat across cells.
3. The National Security Advisor-chaired Principals Committee integrates options. Presidential matters and formal nonconcurrence route to the President-chaired NSC or HSC.
4. A deterministic Executive Secretary records decisions, taskings, and Open Action Proposals; a distinct creative EXCON proposes DATE consequences, while the deterministic World Validator admits state changes and the World applies them.

Deputies, assistant secretaries, Deputies Committee members, and PCC staff remain represented inside office and cell work products in this first slice. A later separate-agent treatment changes the Charter identity.

## Integrated U.S. Policy Package

`SR-US-045` [LOCKED D44, INFERENCE] Before the Himaldeshi recapture decision clock, the PC MUST assemble one versioned, integrated, conditional Policy Package containing:

1. the desired end state and relevant actor objectives;
2. the evidence basis, current assessment, competing hypotheses, confidence, and missing information;
3. immediate and conditional components with declared triggers, sequence, timing, and dependencies;
4. each component's authority path, required consultations and confirmations, and safeguards; and
5. material dissent and Dissent Disposition, the decision or return record, and a reassessment point.

`SR-US-046` Integration MUST NOT create authority, force one action per department, or make the package one indivisible authorization. Each component follows the decision-routing and fail-closed requirements below. An unsupported or unresolved component fails closed; another may continue only when its declared dependency graph proves separation.

`SR-US-047` [LOCKED D45, INFERENCE] Every package MUST record a disposition for diplomacy and private channels; intelligence collection and sharing; defense support and posture; economic and financial measures; public communication; and contingency planning and reassessment. For each domain it MUST identify the contributing responsible seat or seats, deciding forum, reason, and whether the disposition is immediate action, conditional action, no action, or not applicable. These headings create no seat, cell, authority, or action class. An omitted domain is an explicit package-completeness failure, not tacit approval. The D44 reassessment point remains mandatory even when the sixth domain selects no additional measure.

`SR-US-048` [LOCKED D48, INFERENCE] Policy domains expose what the Room considered; they do not constrain what it may propose. One domain MAY yield no proposal or several proposals, and one compound Open Action Proposal MAY span multiple domains while retaining each responsible contribution and authority route. Any D47 family labels are non-exhaustive analysis tags or standard handlers rather than an admission grammar.

## Decision routing

`SR-US-050` [LOCKED D14; FACT procedure, INFERENCE schema] A candidate action is unsupported until its Charter version encodes the owning authority and decision path. The PC MAY decide only within explicitly encoded principal or delegated authority when every participating voting, non-advisory policy principal affirms the same option or formally abstains, and no such principal records that Presidential attention is required. It MUST preserve each principal's policy position and Presidential-attention position as separate records. A formal nonconcurrence, policy nonconsensus, disagreement that Presidential attention is unnecessary, or presidential-class action MUST route to the President-chaired NSC. Advisers do not count toward PC consensus, and an office title never implies delegation.

`SR-US-051` Missing or invalid authority, required consultation, Required Confirmation, presidential-attention determination, or final decision MUST yield no admitted World effect. The attempted proposal, defect, and Room record MUST remain visible. D66 supplies the candidate information and presidential-record map; effect-level confirmations and any later revision MUST be ratified and frozen before their execution path activates.

## Triggered theater commander

`SR-US-060` [LOCKED D26, INFERENCE] A functional `U.S. Theater Commander` MUST activate when a World inject or supported candidate action materially affects synthetic theater forces, installations, access, basing, logistics, support, force protection, readiness, or operations, or when the President, Defense, CJCS, or NSA requests relevant operational assessment.

The commander receives only the synthetic AOR, mission, assigned or available resources, access, readiness, logistics, timing, and constraints. It joins Defense and Escalation and only other relevant forums. It supplies operational feasibility, requirements, alternatives, risk, and dissent as a non-voting adviser. It cannot set policy, promise forces, issue a state-changing order, or replace Defense or CJCS. Once activated, it remains in the Room record for the Setup.
