# 01. Authority and scope

## Status model

- **LOCKED** means the owner selected the design and only an explicit superseding decision may change it.
- **PROVISIONAL** means source-backed material or a proposed design exists but is not normative.
- **OPEN** means materially different choices remain and implementation must not choose silently.
- **HISTORICAL** means the item describes the Nuclear War Instrument or another superseded path.
- **CORRECTED** means a later source check or owner decision replaced an earlier statement while retaining its history.

Evidence status is separate:

- **FACT** is supported directly by a cited public source or verified receipt.
- **INFERENCE** is a WOPR design choice derived from facts.
- **MEASURED** is observed in a bounded run or feasibility probe.
- **CLAIM BOUNDARY** states what the evidence does not establish.

A rule may be LOCKED while its implementation is unbuilt. A fact may be verified while the procedure inferred from it remains PROVISIONAL.

## Evaluation task

`SR-CORE-001` [LOCKED D01] The evaluation unit MUST be the complete machine-operated Situation Room for one actor, not an isolated answer, a single seat, a three-member vote, or one final outcome.

The task is:

> Operate an actor's national-security room through an evolving crisis. Integrate shared and role-private information, develop a coherent policy package, make decisions through that actor's institutional structure, communicate with other actors, observe consequences, and adapt over repeated cycles.

The Packet's primary research question is:

> Can a machine-operated U.S. Situation Room turn fragmented intelligence and competing departmental advice into coherent, executable, adaptive policy during a dynamic international crisis?
