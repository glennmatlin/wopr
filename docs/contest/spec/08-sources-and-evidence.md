# 08. Sources and evidence

## Evidence classes

`SR-EVID-001` Every human-facing and machine-facing design claim MUST identify one of four evidence classes:

- `FACT`: a dated statement supported by a cited public source or verified repository record;
- `INFERENCE`: our explicit operationalization or interpretation of one or more facts;
- `MEASURED`: observed behavior bound to code, environment, inputs, and result;
- `CLAIM BOUNDARY`: an explicit statement of what the supporting evidence does not establish.

`LOCKED`, `PROVISIONAL`, `OPEN`, `HISTORICAL`, and `CORRECTED` remain design-status labels, not evidence classes.

A public fact does not make our machine procedure official. A seam receipt does not prove an end-to-end Room. A locked design does not prove implementation.

## Comparative literature and historical engineering

The design record retains [Human vs. Machine](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.03407), [Biziouras](https://doi.org/10.1080/15512169.2013.770987), [The Situation Room Game](https://activelearningps.com/category/simulations-and-games/), [Open-Ended Wargames with Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.11446), [Shall We Play a Game?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.17192), and [SIGNAL](https://doi.org/10.1177/00223433221094734) as comparators. They motivate differentiated institutions, dossiers, private briefs, role-specific openness, injects, and auditable consequence control; they are not evidence that this Room design works. The Situation Room Game is a teaching-game comparator, not a peer-reviewed empirical result.

`SR-EVID-030` The Nuclear War Instrument receipts establish only the behavior actually observed in their frozen manifests and traces, including a structured seat mechanism, corrected release-rule behavior, replay-linked action, and one composed Nuclear War Room-to-World path. They do not establish population effects, institutional superiority, realistic crisis physics, the D48 same-U.S.-Room cross-World run, a DATE run, or an admissible Overlay Demo.

`SR-EVID-031` Every source, inference, receipt, correction, failure, and non-claim used in the microsite MUST remain inspectable. Failed probes and corrected misunderstandings MUST be preserved rather than silently removed.

`SR-EVID-032` [LOCKED D61] These comparators locate the entry within LLM strategic and nuclear-crisis simulation work. The intended addition is inspectable actor-specific institutions, selective information, coherent fictional state, open policy, state-bounded consequences, matching, and replay. Until a source-backed comparison and receipt-valid runs exist, this is a design distinction rather than evidence of superior authenticity, quality, or outcomes.
