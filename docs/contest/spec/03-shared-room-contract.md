# 03. Shared Room contract

## Room Charter

`SR-CHARTER-001` [LOCKED D06] Each actor MUST have a versioned, actor-specific Room Charter. A Crisis Setup MAY activate and brief registered institutions, but MUST NOT invent a seat, group, mandate, authority, or confirmation rule.

Every Charter MUST define:

- actor identity, Charter version, and content hash;
- public-source and design-inference cards;
- actor objective vector and hard admissibility constraints;
- complete seat and group registry;
- each seat's mandate, persona, information entitlement, supported contributions, and prohibited actions;
- each group's members, purpose, inputs, outputs, triggers, and dependencies;
- communication and selective-disclosure permissions;
- action-class decision routing, required consultation, and Required Confirmations;
- activation predicates and persistence rules;
- deterministic services and failure behavior.

`SR-PERSONA-001` [LOCKED D16] A persona MUST be office-grounded, versioned, and fixed across matched Setups and Seeds. It MAY contain public doctrine, dated posture, analytic emphasis, communication behavior, and bounded professional risk concerns. It MUST NOT contain random temperament, decorative biography, or a private victory condition.

## Shared outer cycle

`SR-CYCLE-001` [LOCKED D03] Every actor MUST use these outer checkpoints:

1. The World distributes a frozen Common Crisis Picture and authorized private briefs.
2. Activated groups work under their Room Charter.
3. The responsible forum records structured products, Policy Package, material dissent, uncertainty, and missing information.
4. The Room records decided action components as World-contract-specific Open Action Proposals or closed legal moves and requests clarification when required.
5. The World contract adjudicates and validates the decision, advances state and MSEL, returns consequences, and begins the next cycle. DATE uses distinct creative EXCON and deterministic World Validator roles; Nuclear War uses its finite legal-move and rules-engine path.

`SR-CYCLE-002` [LOCKED D53 FOR FIRST DATE EPISODE] A complete U.S. Room Cycle begins with an entitled information state and closes only after Charter work, an attributable decision, Open Action Proposals or recorded non-action, adjudication and validation, and delivery of resulting consequences. The first episode MUST complete two such cycles unless a frozen substantive World terminal fires first.

`SR-CYCLE-003` [LOCKED D53 FOR FIRST DATE EPISODE] Cycle 2 MUST reassess the information and World changes returned from Cycle 1 and issue a second attributable decision. The policy MAY remain unchanged when the Room records why, but silent repetition or a skipped decision path MUST NOT count as adaptation.

`SR-CYCLE-004` [LOCKED D54 FOR FIRST DATE EPISODE] The Cycle 2 U.S. information state MUST combine one frozen Matched Pressure Inject with separately sourced endogenous consequences from Cycle 1. Common and divergent facts MUST retain distinct identities, causal parents, delivery records, and memory lineage rather than being merged into an unattributed narrative update.

The number of internal seat turns, retries, review passes, and time budget remains OPEN and MUST be frozen before a run. D53 fixes the first episode's U.S. Room Cycle count, not one model turn per seat or a symmetric cycle count for every Counterpart Room.

## Seats, groups, and activation

`SR-SEAT-001` A distinct model-mediated seat MUST exercise Room judgment that would be lost if collapsed into another seat. Transport, scheduling, recordkeeping, schema validation, and state admission MUST remain deterministic. D48 separately approves a model-mediated EXCON for DATE consequence proposals but does not make it a Room seat or validator.

`SR-SEAT-002` One institutional seat MUST retain one identity, persistent memory, disclosure history, and trace across every group it joins. It MUST NOT be clone-run concurrently.

`SR-GROUP-001` [LOCKED D07] A Group MUST be an operational unit with declared members, mandate, access, triggers, inputs, outputs, and relation to the actor's decision process. A label inside one plenary chat is not a Group.

`SR-ACTIVATE-001` A registered inactive seat MUST make no model call and MUST NOT imply advice, support, opposition, abstention, or confirmation.

`SR-ACTIVATE-002` Initial activation MUST follow frozen Setup predicates and match across compared runs. Later activation MAY follow typed World events, candidate actions, or validated consultation requests.

`SR-ACTIVATE-003` Once activated, a seat MUST remain part of the Room record for the rest of the Setup. Its calls MAY be suppressed when irrelevant, but its memory, earlier advice, and disclosures MUST NOT disappear.

`SR-ACTIVATE-004` A missing registered function MUST produce `CHARTER_GAP`; the controller MUST NOT invent an expert at runtime.

## Information and disclosure

`SR-INFO-001` [LOCKED D04] Role-private information MUST remain private until a Charter-authorized disclosure or a later World event makes it common.

`SR-INFO-002` A disclosure MUST record source seat, recipient, cycle, fact identifiers, content hash, and delivery time. Once delivered, the information MUST enter the recipient's persistent memory.

`SR-INFO-003` The adapter MUST construct a separate authorized input for every seat. It MUST NOT provide a union of private briefs with textual instructions to ignore unauthorized sections.

`SR-INFO-004` A Common Crisis Picture is the actor's shared information state, not hidden World truth and not a guarantee that every included report is reliable.

`SR-INFO-005` [LOCKED D57 FOR FIRST U.S. EPISODE] The Cycle 1 weather forecast MUST follow the U.S. Charter's specialist route and remain outside the general Common Crisis Picture; senior forums receive attributable products and evidence links, while underlying-report access remains seat-specific and logged. The linked Cycle 2 confirmation and changed access state MUST become common to every active U.S. seat.

## Dependency execution and concurrency

`SR-SCHED-001` [LOCKED D08] Independent group or seat work MAY run concurrently only after inputs are frozen, active seat sets are disjoint, and neither node consumes the other's current product.

`SR-SCHED-002` Each concurrent node MUST write an isolated trace. The controller MUST collect results in frozen Charter order rather than wall-clock completion order.

`SR-SCHED-003` A shared seat or product dependency MUST introduce an explicit serialization barrier. Parallel execution MUST NOT change logical inputs, persistent memories, decision ordering, or replay order relative to a serial execution of the same graph.

`SR-SCHED-004` [LOCKED PROCESS D65] The contest-scoped Room controller MAY execute frozen delivery, activation, dependency, scheduling, routing, validation, and artifact rules but MUST NOT supply seat judgment, summarize evidence, repair products, resolve dissent, grant authority, choose policy, propose creative consequences, or mutate World state.

`SR-SCHED-005` A deterministic counterpart fixture MAY support Focal Room development only when its identity and outputs are frozen and labeled non-evidence. It MUST NOT satisfy a Counterpart Room, live episode, or evaluation gate.

`SR-BUDGET-001` Concurrent calls MUST use isolated mutable call budgets and MAY share only synchronized study-level cost accounting.

## Failure boundary

`SR-FAIL-001` Missing or invalid required inputs, products, reviews, decision records, confirmations, proposal meaning, authority, capability, or state-admission fields MUST fail closed for the affected effect. Novelty or lack of a standard handler MUST NOT erase an otherwise intelligible Open Action Proposal.

`SR-FAIL-002` An unrelated component MAY continue only when the deterministic dependency graph proves separation. Ambiguity is not proof of separation.

`SR-FAIL-003` World time and authored consequences of delay MAY advance after a Room failure or deadlock; the controller MUST NOT repair institutional disagreement by manufacturing authority or consent.
