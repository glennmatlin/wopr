01 / Problem CLAIM BOUNDARY

Single-agent crisis simulations collapse three jobs.

One model is often asked to decide what a Room recommends, narrate what the World did, and judge whether its own trace is acceptable. A plausible paragraph can then hide authority, causality, and evidence.

WOPR separates those jobs. The Room owns institutional deliberation. A constrained interpreter preserves the proposal without granting it force. The World owns state and consequences, while replay binds each decision to what came before.

Design claim / not a result

The goal is an inspectable decision path, not a one-number model ranking.

03 / DATE / Primary World PROPOSED / OPEN

Episode 1 starts with a regional crisis, not a U.S. war.

The first DATE Setup is an abstract Himaldesh-Olvana occupation and recapture episode. Nuclear escalation remains reachable but is not scripted. The U.S. Room responds to what it can establish, not to hidden World truth.

STARTEX

Occupation and an uncertain request

Himaldesh asks for bounded support while the U.S. receives fragmented, source-linked information about intent, access, and the recapture clock.

CYCLE 1

Specialist products become policy

Watch routes the warning through Threat and Attribution and Defense and Escalation. Seats make recommendations, dissent remains attributable, and the Room may issue an open proposal.

BARRIER

One matched pressure, two dependencies

A High-Altitude Access Degradation is forecast with uncertainty, then confirmed as a common World event. It narrows a U.S.-enabled observation window and a Himaldeshi logistics window without prescribing a response.

CYCLE 2

Reassess what changed

The confirmed state reaches active seats. EXCON and the validator retain causal parents, World reads and writes, admission or rejection, and the difference between a normal horizon, a substantive terminal, and an invalid abort.

04 / Method LOCKED DATE CONTRACT

Open proposals, constrained interpretation, World-owned consequences.

DATE does not force every response into an exhaustive action menu. It does force every handoff to declare what it means, who can authorize it, and whether it changed typed World state.

01

Open Action Proposal

The Room preserves the original proposal and its actor, intended effect, means, object, timing, conditions, and authority path.

02

Constrained interpreter

Clarification and typing make a proposal inspectable. They do not rewrite it, choose for the Room, or turn open prose into state.

03

EXCON and World Validator

A distinct World-side process may propose downstream effects. The validator admits or blocks them against causal parents, evidence, capabilities, and frozen state.

04

MSEL and replay

An admitted consequence enters the World Event Ledger; a Core change carries a typed State Patch. Rejected effects and non-action stay visible.

FACT / LOCKED EXCON can be creative about downstream consequences, but it cannot invent prior intent, authority, capabilities, or Room consent to make a proposal succeed.

06 / Proposed evaluation OPEN / NOT EXECUTED

Measure the decision path and the World outcome separately.

After the DATE gates close, matched Setup and Seed conditions can test whether the Room turns changing information into coherent, attributable policy. The design keeps institutional performance distinct from what the World eventually does.

Institutional Performance Vector

How the Room handled the case

  • Information arrival, activation, and evidence requests
  • Recommendations, dissent, clarification, and disposition
  • Policy Package coverage, authority, confirmation, and reassessment
  • Replay integrity, non-action, delay, and failure classification
World Outcome Vector

What the validated World recorded

  • Admitted ledger events and typed Core transitions
  • Observation, logistics, readiness, and dependency changes
  • Ordinary or forced escalation as separate downstream categories
  • Terminal state or bounded two-cycle endpoint
Claim boundary

A sound process can meet an adverse World result, and a favorable result can follow a deficient process. Neither vector becomes a universal score or a cross-World ranking.
