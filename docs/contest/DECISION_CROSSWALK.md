## Completeness rule

The crosswalk accounts for 90 primary records. Provisional actor research, seam receipts, evidence boundaries, Packet status, and unresolved gates are normalized in spec modules 07-19. Future decisions MUST append to the continuation, preserve every considered option and disposition, update the relevant ADR or spec, add a crosswalk row, and update the Setup option register when an alternative is crisis-semantic; a decision is not durable if it exists only in chat.
