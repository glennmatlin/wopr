# Situation Room Setup option register

Status: selected first-Setup spine is LOCKED; retained candidates and exact identifiers remain PROVISIONAL

This register keeps crisis-semantic alternatives and bounded Seed axes available for future authoring without turning every design alternative into a Crisis Setup. The ADR and continuation preserve the full reasoning; this file records each arena-relevant disposition.

## Classification contract

| Classification | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Selected Setup contract | Locked content of the first runnable Setup |
| Candidate Setup family | A distinct opening pattern or strategic-intent family retained for authoring |
| Candidate Setup identity | A distinct authored case inside a family, including different actor intent, information shape, or political pressure |
| World Seed axis | Bounded friction inside one Setup that cannot change its identity |
| In-Setup policy possibility | A Room choice that remains available without changing the authored crisis identity |
| Non-Setup alternative | An implementation choice or rejected design preserved in its ADR and the decision crosswalk |

Every documented design decision must retain all considered options and classify them. Selected contracts, candidate families or identities, and retained World Seed axes belong in this register; in-Setup policy possibilities and non-Setup alternatives remain in their ADR and crosswalk. Exact IDs become authoritative only when the Setup is authored, hashed, and frozen.

## Retained World Seed axes

Within a frozen Setup, bounded detection timing, report timing, background weather, readiness, sensor confidence, contradiction, communications friction, and operational delay may vary by recorded Seed. The weather event, forecast, paired dependency classes, mixed ownership, atomic direct patch, confirmation, conventional opening, and reachable nuclear path required by D55-D61 are Setup content: a Seed cannot change their identity, timing, content, applicability, patch, entitlement, or observation. A Seed also cannot switch among any family, information shape, dependency bundle, ownership profile, partner posture, or escalation shape listed above. Module 13 selects one no-variation default Seed for the first comparison; matched comparisons always freeze it.
