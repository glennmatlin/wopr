# Situation Room contest

Language for the ChinaTalk evaluation design. The primary simulator is a DATE-backed Himaldesh-Olvana conflict with nuclear escalation reachable but not scripted; the Packet evaluates one complete machine-operated U.S. national-security Room responding to it, then reuses that Room in the closed Nuclear War game World.
## Arena

**World**:
The versioned reality and consequence system outside a Room, represented by a typed World Core and causal World Event Ledger. Each World defines its own Room-World Contract.
_Avoid_: scenario, prompt, unconstrained game master
**World Core**:
The episode-bounded typed state of entities, control and status, ground truth, affordances, active commitments, clocks, terminal conditions, and outcome projections that deterministic World mechanics query or change. It is deliberately incomplete, and an absent fact is neither false nor permission to invent it.
_Avoid_: complete world model, narrative state, consequence catalog
**World Affordance**:
An existing typed capability, resource, access path, or relationship that supports what an actor may attempt or a consequence may use. It does not grant authority or guarantee an effect.
_Avoid_: legal move, automatic success, invented capability
**Force Package**:
A named fictional operational aggregate whose typed location, control, posture, readiness, access, support dependencies, and broad capability bands are relevant to the episode. It is not a real formation or weapon inventory.
_Avoid_: order of battle, strategic token, real unit analogue
**World Event Ledger**:
The append-only causal chronology of admitted exogenous events, consequences, and observations. Its envelope is fixed while its content may remain open; an entry cannot silently change the World Core.
_Avoid_: transcript, exhaustive event catalog, mutable truth
**Room-World Contract**:
The versioned boundary defining what a Room observes and decides, how proposals or legal moves enter adjudication, what becomes World state, and which evidence is retained.
_Avoid_: universal action grammar, shared consequence engine
**Crisis Setup**:
An authored crisis identity inside the World, including the Road to War, STARTEX physical and information state, actor intent, partner-request category, opening incident, private briefs, constraints, and MSEL graph.
_Avoid_: seed, vignette as the whole arena, random scenario
**Crisis Setup Family**:
A catalog grouping for distinct Crisis Setups that share a recognizable opening pattern while retaining separate identities when actor intent or causal branches differ.
_Avoid_: seed family, interchangeable variant, one Setup with randomized intent
**Authoring Default**:
A versioned, reversible first-episode value selected under delegated authoring authority, recorded before outputs, and frozen with a profile and hash without becoming a locked scientific or Charter decision.
_Avoid_: unrecorded convenience, owner-ratified claim, Seed
**World Seed**:
A recorded choice that varies bounded uncertainty and friction inside one Crisis Setup without changing doctrine, political objectives, or strategic intent.
_Avoid_: setup, personality seed, intent seed
**MSEL**:
The versioned exercise-event structure containing authored exogenous events plus traceable endogenous injects admitted from adjudication.
_Avoid_: random event deck, unrecorded model invention
**Matched Pressure Inject**:
One frozen Setup-authored exogenous MSEL development whose identity, trigger, exogenous fact, and entitled U.S. observation are held constant across matched normal runs that reach it. It supplements rather than replaces each run's endogenous consequences.
_Avoid_: fixed trajectory, state reset, hidden answer key
**High-Altitude Access Degradation**:
The first Setup's selected Matched Pressure Inject: one exogenous weather deterioration that jointly restricts a predeclared observation or ISR window and a separate logistics or support window. An uncertain Cycle 1 forecast precedes their matched confirmation and atomic state change at the Cycle 2 barrier.
_Avoid_: random weather seed, unwarned surprise, perfect advance knowledge, forced operational outcome
**Episode Horizon**:
The normal bounded endpoint of one Crisis Setup run, distinct from an earlier substantive World terminal and from an invalid run abort. The first DATE episode's horizon is two complete U.S. Room Cycles.
_Avoid_: victory condition, forced crisis resolution, model timeout
**EXCON**:
The distinct World-side adjudicator that may propose unforeseen downstream consequences from recorded causal parents and current state, but may not rewrite prior state, invent enabling capabilities or intent, or decide for a represented Room.
_Avoid_: hidden adviser, Room member, state validator
## Institution

**Room**:
One actor's complete machine-operated national-security decision organization, including its groups, seats, information paths, decisions, and adaptation across crisis cycles. A deterministic controller may execute its Charter but cannot supply institutional judgment.
_Avoid_: three-agent council, one plenary chat, isolated recommendation, controller as Room
**Room Cycle**:
One actor-scoped passage from entitled information through Charter work and an attributable decision, proposal or recorded non-action, then World adjudication and returned consequences. A later cycle begins from that resulting information state.
_Avoid_: model turn, chat round, one seat response
**Focal Room**:
The Room whose model or system condition is varied and whose causal trace anchors the Packet's primary evaluation and public claim. The initial Focal Room is the United States.
_Avoid_: most convenient implementation target, sole actor in the World, generic lead agent
**Counterpart Room**:
An actor-specific Room whose fixed system configuration still observes, decides, communicates, and reacts around the Focal Room without becoming a co-equal submission target. A deterministic development fixture is neither a Counterpart Room nor evidence.
_Avoid_: scripted adversary, development fixture, country skin, second focal benchmark
**Room Charter**:
The frozen, source-card-backed definition of one actor's institutions, objectives, groups, seats, mandates, information entitlements, activation rules, and decision paths.
_Avoid_: country skin, setup-authored organization, universal Room template
**Institution Registry**:
The complete set of seats and groups that a Room Charter permits, whether or not each one is active in a particular Crisis Setup.
_Avoid_: active roster, setup cast list
**Seat**:
A persistent judgment-bearing institutional role with one mandate, information history, and identity across every group it joins.
_Avoid_: character, interchangeable agent, cloned office
**Mandate**:
The professional responsibility that determines what a seat must assess, protect, contribute, or challenge.
_Avoid_: private objective, personality, biography
**Persona**:
A versioned public posture covering doctrine, analytic emphasis, communication behavior, and bounded professional risk concerns.
_Avoid_: random temperament, covert victory condition, officeholder imitation
**Group**:
A Charter-defined working or decision forum with members, purpose, information access, activation conditions, and a declared product.
_Avoid_: label inside one global chat, runtime-invented committee
**Activation**:
The recorded transition that brings a registered seat or group into the active Room when a frozen setup, event, action, or consultation predicate is satisfied.
_Avoid_: attendance by model preference, implied participation
## Information and work products
**Common Crisis Picture**:
The versioned facts, reports, uncertainties, and disclosed developments that every currently entitled seat in one actor's Room may receive.
_Avoid_: ground truth, omniscient summary, union of private briefs
**Portfolio Brief**:
The World-authored private information assigned to one portfolio under its Charter entitlement for a Crisis Setup and phase.
_Avoid_: persona prompt, model-generated evidence
**Portfolio Product**:
A structured, attributable portfolio assessment that states its position, evidence basis, uncertainty, blockers, and coordination needs without exposing all raw private material by default.
_Avoid_: vote, transcript summary, state-changing action
**Policy Package**:
The versioned Room output that states intended ends, evidence, uncertainty, required policy-domain dispositions, action components, dependencies, safeguards, dissent, and decision records. Each required domain records immediate action, conditional action, no action, or not applicable with an attributable reason, while the reassessment point remains required.
_Avoid_: free-form final answer, automatic World action, one-action-per-department quota
**Portfolio Review**:
A portfolio's version-bound professional position on the parts of a Policy Package that affect its mandate.
_Avoid_: veto, vote total, Required Confirmation
**Dissent Disposition**:
The decision principal's attributable response to advisory opposition, recording whether it was adopted, mitigated, narrowed, withdrawn, or carried forward with an explicit unresolved risk.
_Avoid_: relabeling dissent as concurrence, silent override
**Required Confirmation**:
A Charter-authored answer to a bounded prerequisite for one typed action component, distinct from whether the confirming seat supports the policy.
_Avoid_: general portfolio veto, symmetry gate, self-declared blocker
## Action, evidence, and submission
**Open Action Proposal**:
The DATE Room's canonical, attributable proposal preserved in its original language with a minimal envelope for actor, intended effect, means, object or audience, timing, conditions, and decision record. It is not limited to a predeclared action family.
_Avoid_: legal-move ID, exhaustive action type, automatic World effect
**World Validator**:
The deterministic admission boundary that checks a proposed consequence's causal envelope and any linked State Patch against frozen entities, time, authority, capability, and state consistency.
_Avoid_: policy maker, creative adjudicator, plausibility narrator
**State Patch**:
A typed, preconditioned proposal to change the World Core, linked to a causal consequence proposal; it becomes current state only after validation. A ledger-only observation does not require one.
_Avoid_: retroactive rewrite, narrative consequence as truth
**World Consequence**:
An event or observation admitted to the World Event Ledger after an Open Action Proposal, closed legal move, EXCON adjudication, or exogenous event. A consequence that changes the World Core carries an accepted State Patch.
_Avoid_: unvalidated model prediction, Room claim
**Source Card**:
A record separating a public-source fact from the Room Charter inference built from it, with source identity and retrieval context.
_Avoid_: unsupported institutional claim, implied endorsement
**Matched Run**:
One evaluation condition executed against the same frozen World, Crisis Setup, World Seed, exogenous information, and Room Charter inputs as its comparator.
_Avoid_: loosely similar scenario, unmatched free play
**Institutional Performance Vector**:
The trace-derived family of measures describing how a Room handled information, mandates, options, dissent, decision requirements, action mapping, and adaptation, kept distinct from what happened in the World.
_Avoid_: procedure score, consensus score, proof that a good process caused a good outcome
**Outcome Projection**:
A frozen typed view of World Core facts used to report one declared World-outcome dimension. It is neither a score nor a post-hoc interpretation of open ledger prose.
_Avoid_: hidden objective function, after-the-fact metric, overall winner
**World Outcome Vector**:
The declared family of consequences and actor-objective results derived from World Core state and transitions, recorded non-action or delay, and closed legal moves, kept distinct from how well the Room operated.
_Avoid_: winner, de-escalation score, single overall quality score
**Evaluation Profile**:
The paired presentation of a Room's Institutional Performance Vector and World Outcome Vector, linked through the retained information-to-decision-to-action, non-action, or delay-to-consequence record without collapsing either family into one scalar.
_Avoid_: leaderboard score, outcome-only ranking, process-only compliance grade
**Authored Risk Probe**:
A frozen pre-run evaluation annotation that binds a material fact, contradiction, uncertainty, dependency, or risk to relevant actor objectives, Charter mandates, applicability conditions, and observable trace evidence without prescribing a preferred policy.
_Avoid_: hidden answer key, post-hoc rubric, injected Room evidence, model-judge opinion
**Run Identity**:
The complete provenance binding for a replay, including World, Room-World Contract, Setup, Seed, Charters, seating, models, adjudicator, validator, executor, observations, decisions, proposals or moves, and consequences.
_Avoid_: folder name as identity, latest run
**Packet**:
The single contest submission and its public-facing application, protocol, evidence, microsite, repository, and presentation trail.
_Avoid_: multiple unrelated entries, claims beyond receipts
**Closed Nuclear War World**:
The deliberately unrealistic, finite-move WOPR rules engine used as the secondary cross-World stressor for the same U.S. Room and as the source of historical engineering receipts.
_Avoid_: DATE realism evidence, substitute for the open DATE World
