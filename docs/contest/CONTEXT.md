# ChinaTalk Situation Room glossary

Status: active language for the two-World design, not a claim that the live evaluation is complete. DATE is the primary authenticity-oriented Himaldesh-Olvana crisis lane. Nuclear War is the deliberately closed secondary lane. Both use the same defined U.S. Room.

## World and crisis

- **World:** The versioned reality and consequence system outside a Room, including its typed Core, causal Event Ledger, Room-World Contract, action protocol, adjudication, and replay.
- **Room-World Contract:** The boundary over what a Room observes and decides, how proposals or legal moves enter adjudication, what becomes World truth, and which evidence is retained. It is not a universal action grammar or shared consequence engine.
- **Crisis Setup:** A frozen episode identity containing the Road to War, STARTEX, opening physical and information state, actor intent, partner requests, private briefs, constraints, clocks, and MSEL graph. It is distinct from a World Seed.
- **Crisis Setup Family:** A versioned family that groups distinct authored Setups without treating them as seed variations of one scenario.
- **Authoring Default:** Reversible, versioned synthetic first-episode content used when the design delegates a bounded choice. It is not an observed fact.
- **World Seed:** A recorded choice that varies bounded uncertainty and friction inside one Setup without changing doctrine, political objectives, strategic intent, or Setup identity.
- **MSEL:** The episode's master event sequence, containing authored exogenous events and traceable admitted endogenous events.
- **Force Package:** An abstract fictional operational aggregate used to represent episode capabilities and dispositions without asserting a real order of battle.
- **World Core:** Typed episode ground truth queried by mechanics and declared measures, including entities, control, status, affordances, commitments, clocks, escalation, and terminal conditions. An absent fact is neither false nor permission to invent it.
- **Outcome Projection:** A read-only typed view derived from the World Core for evaluation or display. It cannot alter state.
- **World Event Ledger:** The append-only causal chronology of admitted exogenous events, observations, and consequences. Every entry carries provenance, and ledger prose cannot silently change Core truth.
- **World Affordance:** A typed capability, resource, access path, or relationship supporting an attempted action or consequence. It grants neither authority nor guaranteed success.
- **State Patch:** A typed, preconditioned proposal to change the World Core. It becomes current state only after deterministic validation.
- **Matched Pressure Inject:** A frozen Setup-authored event whose identity, trigger, observation, and direct Core patch remain fixed across matched normal runs while endogenous consequences remain separate.
- **High-Altitude Access Degradation:** The first episode's matched weather pressure, forecast with uncertainty before it narrows separate U.S. observation and Himaldeshi support windows.
- **Episode Horizon:** The first normal episode ends after two complete U.S. Room Cycles unless a substantive terminal condition occurs. An invalid abort is not a substantive terminal.

## Institution

- **Room:** One actor's complete machine-operated national-security decision organization, including persistent seats, groups, information paths, decisions, and adaptation across cycles. A deterministic fixture is not institutional judgment.
- **Room Cycle:** Entitled information followed by Charter work, an attributable decision, proposal, or non-action, World adjudication and validation, and returned consequences that persist into the next cycle.
- **Focal Room:** The Room whose model or system condition varies and whose trace anchors the primary evaluation. The first Focal Room is the United States.
- **Counterpart Room:** An actor-specific Room that observes, decides, communicates, and reacts around the Focal Room without becoming a co-equal submission target.
- **Room Charter:** The frozen definition of one actor's objectives, institutions, seats, groups, mandates, information entitlements, activation rules, products, and decision paths.
- **Institution Registry:** The complete set of seats and groups permitted by one Charter. Runtime execution may activate registered elements but cannot invent new ones.
- **Seat:** A persistent judgment-bearing institutional role with one mandate, authorized information history, and identity across every group it joins.
- **Persona:** A versioned, office-grounded judgment posture for one seat. It is not a hidden objective or substitute for the seat's mandate.
- **Mandate:** The professional responsibility determining what a seat must assess, protect, contribute, or challenge. It is not a hidden personal goal.
- **Activation:** A recorded predicate-driven transition that activates a registered seat or group. It does not permit runtime invention.
- **Group:** A Charter-defined working or decision forum with members, purpose, information access, activation conditions, dependencies, and a declared product.

## Information and work products

- **Watch:** The deterministic service that delivers exact authorized bytes and records delivery. It does not interpret evidence or supply judgment.
- **Common Crisis Picture:** Shared entitled crisis information. It is neither hidden World truth nor the union of every role-private brief.
- **Portfolio Brief:** World-authored private information delivered to one portfolio under the Charter's entitlement rules.
- **Portfolio Product:** An attributable portfolio assessment stating its position, evidence, uncertainty, blockers, and coordination needs without automatically exposing all private source material.
- **Portfolio Review:** A seat's attributable review of an integrated product. Its position is separate from answering a Required Confirmation.
- **Dissent Disposition:** The recorded handling of advisory opposition, including whether it was integrated, retained, deferred, or rejected and by whom.
- **Policy Package:** The integrated Room output containing intended ends, evidence, uncertainty, policy-domain dispositions and reasons, dependencies, safeguards, dissent, decision records, and reassessment requirements.
- **Required Confirmation:** A Charter-defined answer to a bounded prerequisite for one action component. It is distinct from whether the confirming seat supports the whole policy.

## Action and evaluation

- **Open Action Proposal:** DATE's attributable natural-language proposal, preserved with a minimal envelope for actor, intended effect, means, object or audience, timing, conditions, and decision record. It is not limited to a predeclared action family.
- **EXCON:** The distinct World-side adjudicator that may propose causally supported unforeseen consequences from recorded parents and current state. It may not decide for a represented Room, rewrite prior state, or invent authority, capability, or intent.
- **World Validator:** The deterministic admission boundary checking meaning, authority, capability, causality, time, patch preconditions, and state consistency.
- **World Consequence:** An admitted ledger event or observation following an Open Action Proposal, legal move, EXCON proposal, or exogenous event. A Core-changing consequence carries an accepted State Patch.
- **Source Card:** A versioned evidence record separating a public source fact from the Charter inference derived from it.
- **Matched Run:** A comparator run that fixes the World, Setup, Seed, Charters, and exogenous inputs while changing only the declared condition.
- **Institutional Performance Vector:** Trace-derived measures for information handling, mandate coverage, uncertainty, dissent, dependencies, authority, risk handling, products, and adaptation. It remains separate from World outcomes.
- **World Outcome Vector:** Typed measures for World state, commitments, control, escalation, affordances, terminal conditions, and recorded non-action or delay. It remains causally linked to, but distinct from, institutional performance.
- **Evaluation Profile:** The declared measurement contract pairing separate Institutional Performance and World Outcome vectors without collapsing either into one scalar score.
- **Authored Risk Probe:** A pre-run annotation binding a material fact, contradiction, uncertainty, dependency, or risk to objectives, Charter mandates, applicability rules, and observable trace evidence without prescribing a preferred policy.
- **Run Identity:** The provenance binding for a replay, including World, Contract, Setup, Seed, seating, Charters, models, executor, adjudicator, validator, observations, decisions, proposals or legal moves, and consequences.
- **Closed Nuclear War World:** The finite-move WOPR engine used as the same-U.S.-Room secondary stressor and source of historical engineering evidence. It is never DATE realism evidence.
- **Packet:** One contest submission and its public evidence trail: one title, abstract, microsite, repository, application, and receipt-bound claim set.

## Historical packet language

The earlier Nuclear War-only packet used `Instrument`, `Preset`, `Four-room table`, `Release rule`, `Sounding`, `Overlay`, and `Demo trajectory` for a three-member C2 study. Those terms remain valid inside its historical receipts but do not define the current complete-Room protocol.
