# Current ChinaTalk design

Status: active design summary as of 2026-08-30. This document defines the
current scientific object but does not claim that the complete evaluation has
run.

## Umbrella question

> Can the same machine-operated U.S. Situation Room produce coherent,
> attributable, adaptive decisions in both an open-ended DATE crisis and the
> deliberately closed Nuclear War game?

The public compression is: "Same Room, two Worlds: one open crisis, one closed
game, every decision traceable."

## Evaluation unit

The evaluation unit is one persistent, complete U.S. national-security Room.
It is not one model response, a three-member vote, an isolated recommendation,
or the final outcome of a game.

A versioned Room Charter defines the eligible seats, mandates, prohibited
actions, information entitlements, activation rules, groups, dependencies,
products, decision routes, required confirmations, and failure effects. The
first U.S. Charter has 20 eligible seats, with 14 active in the first DATE
episode. Seat identity and memory persist across groups and crisis cycles.

The deterministic controller may transport authorized bytes, schedule work,
validate schemas, route products, and retain records. It may not supply
institutional judgment, repair a missing product with its own prose, decide for
a represented Room, or invent World truth.

## Two World contracts

| Property | DATE | Nuclear War |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Role | Primary crisis lane | Secondary entertainment contrast |
| Action interface | Natural-language Open Action Proposals | Finite legal moves |
| Consequences | State-bounded generative EXCON plus deterministic admission | Deterministic game engine |
| State | Typed World Core, open Event Ledger, typed State Patches | Engine state and replay |
| Main pressure | Open policy formation, uncertainty, synthesis, and adaptation | Closed action ontology, hidden information, and engine-owned effects |
| Claim boundary | Authenticity-oriented, not fully realistic | Deliberately unrealistic |

The Room remains the same institution across the two contracts. The World may
change the observation format, action interface, consequence process, terminal
conditions, and World-specific outputs. Those changes must be declared rather
than hidden inside a shared prompt.

## Room cycle

1. The World and Setup create common and role-private information.
2. A deterministic Watch delivers exact authorized bytes and records delivery.
3. Persistent seats produce attributable Portfolio Products.
4. Charter-defined groups integrate those products in dependency order.
5. Senior integration produces one Policy Package with evidence, uncertainty,
   dependencies, safeguards, dissent, and decision records.
6. Encoded authority produces Open Action Proposals, legal moves, or recorded
   non-action and delay.
7. The World contract adjudicates the decision without deciding for the Room.
8. Deterministic validation admits or rejects World events and state changes.
9. Consequences return to the same seats for reassessment.

## DATE open-endedness

DATE preserves the exact original language of each Open Action Proposal with a
minimal attributable envelope: actor, intended effect, means, object or
audience, timing, conditions, source package component, and decision record.
Policy domains and standard handlers may help analysis, but they are not an
exhaustive action grammar.

A distinct EXCON may propose unforeseen downstream consequences from admitted
state and recorded causal parents. It may not invent enabling capability,
authority, evidence, intent, or prior state. A deterministic World Validator
checks meaning, authority, capability, causality, time, patch preconditions,
and state consistency. Novelty alone is not a rejection reason. Rejected and
unresolved proposals remain visible in the trace.

Mechanically consequential truth is separated into three layers:

- The World Core holds the typed facts that future mechanics and declared
  measures query.
- The World Event Ledger retains an open-content causal chronology.
- State Patches propose typed, preconditioned Core changes linked to a causal
  consequence.

## First DATE episode

The first episode is a bounded Himaldesh-Olvana ridge seizure. Olvana has taken
Kestrel Ridge and Talus Node while its wider intent remains unresolved to the
U.S. Room. Himaldesh requests urgent consultation and bounded support before a
decision clock for a limited recapture. Nuclear escalation is reachable through
state and action, but it is neither required nor preferred.

The normal horizon is two complete U.S. Room cycles. An uncertain high-altitude
weather forecast reaches specialist paths in Cycle 1. At the barrier before
Cycle 2, a matched weather deterioration narrows separate U.S. observation and
Himaldeshi support windows. Endogenous consequences remain separate from that
shared pressure. The second cycle must contain attributable reassessment and a
second decision.

Himaldesh and Olvana use actor-specific counterpart Rooms so their decisions
are attributable. They remain supporting actors around the focal U.S. Room and
are not co-equal submission targets.

## Evaluation logic

Matched DATE runs hold the World, Setup, Seed, Charters, counterpart systems,
information schedule, horizon, matched pressure, Core and ledger contracts,
EXCON, validator, and evaluation manifest fixed. Only one declared U.S. model
or system condition changes.

The current default candidate compares normal selective delivery with a
declared authorized-broadcast overlay that gives every active U.S. seat the
same otherwise authorized inputs. That choice is not frozen. Model-family and
integration-ablation alternatives remain documented options.

Each valid run reports two linked but separate families:

- The Institutional Performance Vector describes information reach, mandate
  coverage, uncertainty, dissent, dependencies, authority, risk handling,
  required products, and adaptation.
- The World Outcome Vector describes typed state, control, commitments,
  escalation, affordances, terminals, and recorded non-action or delay.

Authored Risk Probes are frozen before outputs. They test whether a material
fact, contradiction, uncertainty, dependency, or risk became reachable,
surfaced, traveled through an authorized path, entered integration, and
received an attributable disposition. They do not encode a preferred policy.

The vectors are not collapsed into one score. Cross-World comparison is
limited to institutional measures whose meaning and applicability survive both
World contracts. DATE and Nuclear War outcomes are not commensurate.

## Minimum honest completion

The contest demonstration requires one receipt-valid matched DATE comparison
through two complete U.S. cycles, followed by one secondary Nuclear War run
using the same bound U.S. Room. Historical Nuclear War receipts support the
engine lineage, but they do not satisfy the same-Room requirement.
