# Source priority and confidence

1. Official/current Mr. B Games PDFs and product page - highest confidence for current Nuclear War-family rules.
2. Downloaded base rules PDF mirror - high practical value for classic rules but hosted by a third party.
3. Older spinner-era rule scan - strong evidence for older edition components/rules; exact text requires visual inspection or physical copy.
4. BGG moreinfo and Scribd card list/FAQ - useful for names/counts/edge cases; not authoritative for exact wording.
5. VariablePig postal rules - best press/PBM variant source; unofficial.
6. Museum records, news, awards - reliable historical/cultural context, not mechanics authority.
7. Tabletop Simulator/community implementations - useful for comparison, not authority.
8. Wikipedia and retail pages - pointers only.
