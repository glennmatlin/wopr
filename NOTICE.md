# Rights and source material

## The software

The code in this repository is released under the [MIT License](LICENSE).

## The card game

*Nuclear War* is a published card game owned by its publisher. This project
implements a rules engine, not a reproduction of the product. Game mechanics
and rules of play are not themselves copyrightable; their published expression
is.

Accordingly, this repository ships:

- effect summaries and confidence-tracked card metadata derived from public
  sources, with provenance recorded in `research/`, and
- an inventory of card names and counts sufficient to reason about the deck.

It does not ship, and will not accept contributions containing:

- scanned or mirrored copies of any published rulebook,
- transcribed exact card text, or
- reproductions of card art or trade dress.

The private development history contains mirrored rulebook PDFs used as
research inputs. Those files are deliberately excluded from this public
repository. See `docs/source_research_policy.md` for the sourcing rules the
project follows.

## Fictional content

The DATE crisis material (Himaldesh, Olvana, and the associated force packages)
is fictional. Force packages are abstract operational aggregates. Nothing here
asserts a real order of battle, a real government position, or a real
capability of any state.
