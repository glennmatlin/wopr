# Project history

WOPR keeps the current contest entry separate from the engineering record that
produced it. Historical files remain available because the design alternatives,
failed approaches, and reasons for later decisions are part of the project.

## Current entry

- [Contest Packet](docs/contest/README.md)
- [Evidence matrix](docs/contest/EVIDENCE_MATRIX.md)
- [DATE Episode 1](docs/contest/EPISODE_1.md)
- [Deadline pivot](docs/contest/adr/0075-cut-the-experiment-and-package-the-evidence-boundary.md)

These files govern current public claims. In particular, no complete live DATE
evaluation or same-Room Nuclear War comparison is claimed.

## Design record

- [Consolidated Situation Room specification](https://github.com/glennmatlin/wopr/blob/main/docs/contest/SITUATION_ROOM_SPEC.md)
- [Decision crosswalk](https://github.com/glennmatlin/wopr/blob/main/docs/contest/DECISION_CROSSWALK.md)
- [Architecture decision records](docs/contest/adr/)

The design record preserves the alternatives and rationale behind the U.S.-first
Room, open proposals, constrained World admission, two-cycle episode, and
same-Room comparison design. Some documents describe intermediate states; the
deadline pivot and evidence matrix supersede their old execution gates.

## Archived pre-deadline package

The [pre-deadline archive](docs/archive/chinatalk-pre-deadline/README.md)
contains the earlier M2/M3 screening, preflight, promotion,
composition-smoke, and status prose. It is retained for provenance, not as
current instructions or evaluation evidence.

The Nuclear War engine's longer construction chronology remains in the
[`docs/AGENT_HANDOFF.md`](https://github.com/glennmatlin/wopr/blob/main/docs/AGENT_HANDOFF.md)
development record. It no longer serves as the public entry point.
